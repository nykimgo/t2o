# Instruction-Guided and Open-Vocabulary 3D Segmentation for Edit3D Candidate Generation

Research cutoff: 2026-11-12. This note distinguishes reported paper results from Edit3D applicability hypotheses. “Open vocabulary,” “part segmentation,” and benchmark accuracy are not evidence of successful mesh removal, preserved-body safety, or restoration quality.

## What input, output granularity, semantic grounding, proposal alternatives, and source-face mapping does each method support?

### Takeaway
No reviewed system natively solves the full Edit3D problem of taking a source mesh plus a natural-language removal instruction and returning a complete, safe source-face set. The strongest ingredients are complementary: native 3D proposal diversity from P3-SAM and PartField, text grounding from Find3D and scene-level open-vocabulary systems, multi-view instance consolidation from PartSLIP++ and Open3DIS, and explicit object-part hierarchies from Search3D.

### Inferences
- **Applicability hypothesis — G1:** generate a superset before any semantic pruning: P3-SAM’s three masks per point, all automatic prompt masks before NMS, PartField clusters at several hierarchy levels, and a union of native-3D and multi-view masks modeled after Open3DIS. This combines complementary failure modes rather than trusting one segmentation. [P3-SAM](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.06784) [PartField](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Liu_PartField_Learning_3D_Feature_Fields_for_Part_Segmentation_and_Beyond_ICCV_2025_paper.html) [Open3DIS](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/papers/Nguyen_Open3DIS_Open-Vocabulary_3D_Instance_Segmentation_with_2D_Mask_Guidance_CVPR_2024_paper.pdf)
- **Applicability hypothesis — G2:** score each candidate with at least four independent channels: text/part relevance (Find3D or crop features), parent-object compatibility (Search3D), multi-view consistency/completeness (PartSLIP++), and geometry/body-safety terms. Only the first three channels are directly represented in reviewed papers; body preservation is an Edit3D-specific addition. [Find3D](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Ma_Find_Any_Part_in_3D_ICCV_2025_paper.pdf) [Search3D](https://search3d-segmentation.github.io/static/pdf/search3d.pdf) [PartSLIP++](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03015)
- **Applicability hypothesis — source-face contract:** sample points with immutable `(face_id, barycentric_coordinates)` provenance, aggregate point logits and mask membership back to faces, and retain coverage/conflict statistics per face. P3-SAM demonstrates face voting, but none of the text-grounded methods publishes this as a safety-preserving contract. [P3-SAM](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.06784)

### Gaps
- No reviewed release exposes a documented end-to-end contract of `source mesh + free-form instruction -> ranked alternative source-face sets`.
- No benchmark measures whether all repeated/bilateral instructed parts are selected while every body face is preserved.
- P3-SAM’s predicted IoU calibration, especially under domain shift to Edit3D meshes, is not reported.
- PartField’s hierarchy gives alternatives but no official text-grounding head; Find3D gives text scores but no instance hierarchy. Their combination is an integration hypothesis, not a paper result.
- Official runnable releases were not found for OpenPart3D/3D-PU or PS3, and Search3D lacks an explicit root software license.

## Which systems support multiple instances, bilateral or repeated parts, compositional text, open vocabulary, or uncertainty?

### Takeaway
Support is fragmented. Instance methods separate repeated entities, dense text fields support flexible naming, and hierarchical systems capture object-part context, but no system demonstrates reliable conjunction, negation, bilateral quantification, and calibrated alternative-set uncertainty together.

### Cited Findings
- **Multiple instances:** PartSLIP/PartSLIP++ explicitly report part-instance segmentation and mAP@50; P3-SAM automatically partitions a shape into class-agnostic instances; OpenMask3D and Open3DIS separate scene object instances; Search3D and PS3 evaluate part-level instances. [PartSLIP++](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03015) [P3-SAM](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.06784) [OpenMask3D](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/d77b5482e38339a8068791d939126be2-Paper-Conference.pdf) [Open3DIS](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/papers/Nguyen_Open3DIS_Open-Vocabulary_3D_Instance_Segmentation_with_2D_Mask_Guidance_CVPR_2024_paper.pdf) [Search3D](https://search3d-segmentation.github.io/static/pdf/search3d.pdf) [PS3](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026/papers/Yen_PS3_Part_Level_Instance_Segmentation_in_3D_WACV_2026_paper.pdf)
- **Repeated/coplanar parts:** PS3 directly motivates its texture-aware proposals with the failure of geometric over-segmentation to distinguish multiple cabinet doors on the same plane. Its gain is largest at AP50 on MultiScan, while strict AP falls below Search3D on ScanNet++, showing that repetition handling does not imply uniformly better boundaries. [PS3 paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026/papers/Yen_PS3_Part_Level_Instance_Segmentation_in_3D_WACV_2026_paper.pdf)
- **Bilateral completeness:** Find3D can query collective concepts such as “limbs,” and part-instance systems can output separate arms or legs, but none reports an evaluation requiring “remove both/all X” with a count-aware completeness metric. [Find3D paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Ma_Find_Any_Part_in_3D_ICCV_2025_paper.pdf)
- **Open vocabulary:** Find3D is part-level and feed-forward; PartSLIP lifts open-vocabulary 2D detection; OpenScene and ConceptFusion provide per-point open-set features; OpenMask3D/Open3DIS provide object-instance retrieval; Search3D and OpenPart3D explicitly extend text queries to scene parts. [Find3D](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Ma_Find_Any_Part_in_3D_ICCV_2025_paper.pdf) [PartSLIP](https://colin97.github.io/PartSLIP_page/) [OpenScene](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Peng_OpenScene_3D_Scene_Understanding_With_Open_Vocabularies_CVPR_2023_paper.html) [ConceptFusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.07241) [Search3D](https://search3d-segmentation.github.io/static/pdf/search3d.pdf) [OpenPart3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.19331)
- **Compositional text:** Search3D explicitly combines parent-object and child-part scores for phrases such as “seat of a chair.” ConceptFusion supports long-form queries and optional LLM parsing of spatial relations, but explicitly states that its base features lack compositionality and do not understand negation. OpenMask3D shows qualitative free-form relational descriptions, but does not benchmark general logical composition. [Search3D](https://search3d-segmentation.github.io/static/pdf/search3d.pdf) [ConceptFusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.07241) [OpenMask3D](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/d77b5482e38339a8068791d939126be2-Paper-Conference.pdf)
- **Uncertainty/alternatives:** P3-SAM uniquely publishes multiple scale masks plus a learned IoU predictor. PartField and Search3D expose structural alternatives through hierarchies, while Find3D/OpenScene/ConceptFusion expose continuous similarities. OpenMask3D and Open3DIS set all proposal confidences to 1.0 in their reported evaluation, so their AP values do not establish calibrated uncertainty. [P3-SAM](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.06784) [PartField](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Liu_PartField_Learning_3D_Feature_Fields_for_Part_Segmentation_and_Beyond_ICCV_2025_paper.html) [Search3D](https://search3d-segmentation.github.io/static/pdf/search3d.pdf) [OpenMask3D](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/d77b5482e38339a8068791d939126be2-Paper-Conference.pdf) [Open3DIS](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/papers/Nguyen_Open3DIS_Open-Vocabulary_3D_Instance_Segmentation_with_2D_Mask_Guidance_CVPR_2024_paper.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Applicability hypothesis:** G2 should represent instruction semantics as a set constraint, not only a per-mask score: cardinality (`one`, `both`, `all`), spatial qualifier (`left`, `rear`), conjunction, and exclusion should be checked against candidate instances before union. No reviewed paper validates this complete policy.
- **Applicability hypothesis:** disagreement between P3-SAM heads, hierarchy levels, native-3D versus lifted-2D proposals, and viewpoint subsets is a useful empirical uncertainty signal. It is not calibrated probability, so it should trigger E1/E2-style additional observation or local re-segmentation rather than automatic destructive removal. [P3-SAM](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.06784) [Open3DIS](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/papers/Nguyen_Open3DIS_Open-Vocabulary_3D_Instance_Segmentation_with_2D_Mask_Guidance_CVPR_2024_paper.pdf)
- **Applicability hypothesis:** for repeated parts, PS3’s texture/masklet mechanism and PartSLIP++’s cross-view instance matching are more relevant than purely geometric connected components; however, texture similarity can also merge repeated copies, so 3D separation and count constraints remain necessary. [PS3](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026/papers/Yen_PS3_Part_Level_Instance_Segmentation_in_3D_WACV_2026_paper.pdf) [PartSLIP++](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03015)

### Gaps
- No reviewed benchmark tests negated removal instructions, nested relations, or policy constraints such as “remove the two side handles but keep the central handle.”
- No paper reports calibrated probability that an output set is complete, nor separate probabilities for missed target parts and leaked body faces.
- Left/right and other viewpoint-dependent language is vulnerable to coordinate-frame ambiguity; none of the reviewed asset-part benchmarks defines an Edit3D-style canonical user view policy.

## Which datasets and metrics are genuinely comparable, and what limitations matter for attachment removal?

### Takeaway
Comparability is narrow: results are comparable only within the same dataset, split, supervision regime, prompt template, representation, connectivity setting, and metric. Shape-part semantic IoU, class-agnostic decomposition IoU, scene object AP, and scene part AP measure different problems and must not be placed on one performance ladder.

### Cited Findings
- **PartNet/PartNet-E family:** PartSLIP++ uses PartNet-E with 1,906 shapes, 45 categories, and eight labeled shapes per category, reporting semantic mIoU and instance mAP@50. Find3D evaluates zero-shot on PartNet-E and shows strong sensitivity of PartSLIP++ to prompt wording. These settings are comparable only when using the same subset and prompt protocol. [PartSLIP++](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03015) [Find3D](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Ma_Find_Any_Part_in_3D_ICCV_2025_paper.pdf)
- **PartObj-Tiny / PartObj-Tiny-WT:** P3-SAM compares class-agnostic methods with and without mesh connectivity. PartField rises from 53.93 to 79.18 IoU when connectivity and best hierarchy-level selection are used, while watertight and point-cloud settings lack that connectivity shortcut. These numbers should not be mixed. [P3-SAM](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.06784)
- **Find3D’s Objaverse-General and ShapeNetPart-V2:** Objaverse-General evaluates random orientations and held-out object categories; ShapeNetPart-V2 tests domain shift from canonical ShapeNetPart. The benchmark is more relevant to open-world grounding than canonical ShapeNetPart, but still evaluates labeled point segmentation, not removal safety. [Find3D](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Ma_Find_Any_Part_in_3D_ICCV_2025_paper.pdf)
- **ShapeNetPart and FAUST:** SATR’s mIoU gains are semantic segmentation over restricted taxonomies; canonical object orientations and small part vocabularies do not establish open-world attachment handling. [SATR](https://github.com/Samir55/SATR) [Find3D discussion of ShapeNetPart](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Ma_Find_Any_Part_in_3D_ICCV_2025_paper.pdf)
- **ScanNet200, Replica, and S3DIS:** OpenMask3D and Open3DIS report object-instance AP, AP50, and AP25. These data are useful for proposal recall, long-tail text grounding, and multi-instance design, but not for part boundaries or source asset faces. [OpenMask3D](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/d77b5482e38339a8068791d939126be2-Paper-Conference.pdf) [Open3DIS](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/papers/Nguyen_Open3DIS_Open-Vocabulary_3D_Instance_Segmentation_with_2D_Mask_Guidance_CVPR_2024_paper.pdf)
- **MultiScan part benchmark:** Search3D adapts MultiScan to 47 contextual object-part labels. OpenPart3D and PS3 also report MultiScan AP/AP50/AP25, but OpenPart3D fine-tunes its vision decoder on synthetic 3D-PU and changes generated-view input, while PS3 and Search3D explicitly share the same aggregation protocol. The PS3-versus-Search3D comparison is therefore the cleanest direct recent proposal comparison. [Search3D](https://search3d-segmentation.github.io/static/pdf/search3d.pdf) [OpenPart3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.19331) [PS3](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026/papers/Yen_PS3_Part_Level_Instance_Segmentation_in_3D_WACV_2026_paper.pdf)
- **ScanNet++ part annotations:** Search3D’s custom set contains only 14 object and 20 part annotations over eight scenes; PS3 notes that its ScanNet++ test has only 15 labels and is less representative than MultiScan. The reversal between strict AP and AP50/AP25 shows that coarse overlap gains can coexist with poorer high-IoU boundaries. [Search3D](https://search3d-segmentation.github.io/static/pdf/search3d.pdf) [PS3](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026/papers/Yen_PS3_Part_Level_Instance_Segmentation_in_3D_WACV_2026_paper.pdf)
- **3D-PU:** the synthetic dataset has 10,000 scenes and 843,654 annotations, but is assembled from 51,300 shapes in PartNet, SAPIEN, and ShapeNetPart and is constrained to their taxonomies. Its direct-query AP50 of 21.1 versus implicit-query AP50 of 14.3 demonstrates that natural instruction phrasing remains materially harder. [OpenPart3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.19331)
- **UnCoCo:** ConceptFusion’s 78 tabletop objects and multimodal queries test open-set localization, not part instances. Its mIoU cannot be compared with instance AP or class-agnostic decomposition IoU. [ConceptFusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.07241)

### Inferences
- **Applicability hypothesis:** Edit3D needs a dedicated evaluation tuple rather than a single IoU: target-face recall (completeness), body-face precision or leakage rate (safety), instance/count recall, boundary distance or boundary F-score, alternative-set oracle recall at K, and downstream removal/restoration validity. Existing benchmarks provide only subsets of this evidence.
- **Applicability hypothesis:** report both strict and tolerant overlap thresholds. The PS3/Search3D reversal between AP and AP50/AP25 indicates that a method can improve coarse candidate recall while worsening exact boundaries—appropriate for G1, unsafe if mistaken for a final G2/G3 mask. [PS3](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026/papers/Yen_PS3_Part_Level_Instance_Segmentation_in_3D_WACV_2026_paper.pdf)
- **Applicability hypothesis:** connectivity-assisted and best-hierarchy-level results are oracle upper bounds for candidate preservation, not fair final-selection scores. [P3-SAM](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.06784)

### Gaps
- There is no public benchmark that labels detachable versus structural body faces, hidden attachment interfaces, or the post-removal surface that must be restored.
- Existing metrics rarely separate under-removal from over-removal; equal IoU can hide very different safety outcomes.
- Published tests do not measure source-face identity preservation through point sampling, voxelization, rendering, and back-projection.
- Natural-language benchmark coverage is weak for count, bilateral terms, exclusions, and instruction ambiguity.

## Which ideas map directly to G1 versus G2, and which project claims would be unsupported?

### Takeaway
G1 should maximize candidate recall and preserve structured alternatives; G2 should solve a constrained set-selection problem with semantic, instance, completeness, and body-safety evidence. The literature supports these design ingredients separately, but not an end-to-end claim of instruction-correct removal.

### Cited Findings
- **Direct G1 evidence:** P3-SAM deliberately predicts three masks because point prompts are ambiguous in scale, then over-samples automatic prompts before NMS. [P3-SAM](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.06784)
- **Direct G1 evidence:** PartField’s hierarchical clustering supplies multiple granularity levels rather than a single fixed decomposition. [PartField](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Liu_PartField_Learning_3D_Feature_Fields_for_Part_Segmentation_and_Beyond_ICCV_2025_paper.html)
- **Direct G1 evidence:** Open3DIS improves proposal recall by appending 2D-guided proposals to native 3D proposals; the union reaches 73.29% recall on ScanNet200 versus 61.63% for 3D-only proposals. [Open3DIS](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/papers/Nguyen_Open3DIS_Open-Vocabulary_3D_Instance_Segmentation_with_2D_Mask_Guidance_CVPR_2024_paper.pdf)
- **Direct G1 evidence:** PS3’s texture-superpoints and tracked masklets improve AP50 for geometrically similar repeated parts, while its tiny-part failures show why alternatives and additional observations must remain available. [PS3](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026/papers/Yen_PS3_Part_Level_Instance_Segmentation_in_3D_WACV_2026_paper.pdf)
- **Direct G2 evidence:** Search3D’s average of parent-object and child-part similarity outperforms part-only retrieval in its ablation; hierarchical search adds 3.1 AP50 on MultiScan. [Search3D](https://search3d-segmentation.github.io/static/pdf/search3d.pdf)
- **Direct G2 evidence:** PartSLIP++ gains 3.2 mAP@50 from EM-based multi-view consistency over the no-EM variant, supporting cross-view agreement as a selection/refinement signal. [PartSLIP++](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03015)
- **Direct G2 evidence:** Find3D is robust to prompt variation relative to baselines and yields dense text relevance, but its output is semantic rather than instance-aware. [Find3D](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Ma_Find_Any_Part_in_3D_ICCV_2025_paper.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Recommended G1 interface hypothesis:** return a candidate graph, not a flat chosen mask: each node stores source faces, generator, prompt/view provenance, hierarchy parent, scale, connected components, text score vector, quality score, and conflicts/overlaps. Edges encode parent-child, merge, split, overlap, symmetry, and cross-view correspondence.
- **Recommended G2 objective hypothesis:** select a set of candidate nodes that maximizes instruction relevance, requested-instance coverage, parent-object consistency, and cross-view support while penalizing body leakage, unexplained disconnected islands, duplicate coverage, and boundary instability. Keep the runner-up set when the margin is small.
- **Recommended adaptive-policy hypothesis:** trigger re-selection when only semantics disagree; trigger extra views when evidence is occlusion- or visibility-limited; trigger local re-segmentation when candidate unions have good semantic coverage but unstable boundaries. These policies extrapolate from multi-view and hierarchy ablations and are not published Edit3D results. [PartSLIP++](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03015) [OpenPart3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.19331)
- **Unsupported claim:** “Open-vocabulary segmentation proves arbitrary natural-language instruction following.” ConceptFusion explicitly reports missing compositionality and negation, and OpenPart3D performs substantially worse for implicit than direct queries. [ConceptFusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.07241) [OpenPart3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.19331)
- **Unsupported claim:** “State-of-the-art part segmentation proves safe attachment removal.” None of the reviewed metrics evaluates body preservation, topological deletion validity, hidden attachment surfaces, or restoration.
- **Unsupported claim:** “A single confidence score is calibrated uncertainty.” P3-SAM predicts overlap quality, dense methods expose cosine similarity, and OpenMask3D/Open3DIS use fixed confidence 1.0 in evaluation; these scores have different meanings. [P3-SAM](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.06784) [OpenMask3D](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/d77b5482e38339a8068791d939126be2-Paper-Conference.pdf) [Open3DIS](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/papers/Nguyen_Open3DIS_Open-Vocabulary_3D_Instance_Segmentation_with_2D_Mask_Guidance_CVPR_2024_paper.pdf)
- **Unsupported claim:** “Multiple-instance support guarantees both/all repeated parts.” Existing instance AP does not test instruction-conditioned cardinality or bilateral completeness.
- **Unsupported claim:** “Point masks map losslessly to source faces.” Voxelization, sparse sampling, visibility, and back-projection can miss thin or occluded triangles; only P3-SAM documents a face-voting path, and it still flood-fills unlabeled faces. [P3-SAM](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.06784)
- **Unsupported claim:** “Module 2 or the frozen mask interface must change.” The reviewed ideas can be implemented upstream as G1 candidate metadata and G2 set selection; this research provides no evidence requiring a frozen-module change.

### Gaps
- The correct weighting between completeness and body safety is project-specific and requires Edit3D validation data.
- It remains unknown whether preserving pre-NMS P3-SAM/Open3DIS candidates materially improves final instruction success or only increases redundant false positives.
- No reviewed paper defines a principled stopping rule for additional viewpoints versus local 3D re-segmentation.
- Symmetry detection and instruction-aware cardinality need separate validation; they are not solved merely by using an instance segmenter.

## Which official code and checkpoints are available, and what licensing or deployment constraints apply?

### Takeaway
Find3D, OpenScene, OpenMask3D, Open3DIS, ConceptFusion, PartSLIP/PartSLIP++, and SATR have permissively licensed code; P3-SAM and PartField have material use restrictions; Search3D has public code without an explicit root license; and OpenPart3D and PS3 lacked official runnable releases at the cutoff. Dependency and input assumptions are at least as important as code availability.

### Cited Findings
- **Permissive releases:** Find3D is MIT with an MIT model card; OpenMask3D is MIT; Open3DIS is BSD-3-Clause; ConceptFusion is MIT; OpenScene is Apache-2.0; PartSLIP is BSD-3-Clause-Clear; PartSLIP++ and SATR are MIT. Legal review is required before adoption. [License](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-Part/main/LICENSE)

- **Unlicensed/absent release:** Search3D’s official repository is public but has no root LICENSE at the checked revision; OpenPart3D’s project page has no code/checkpoint/data download; PS3’s CVF record has no code link. - **Checkpoint caveat:** an available checkpoint may not reproduce the strongest paper setting. PartField withholds its PartNet-trained model, PartSLIP++ notes that the released PartSLIP checkpoint underperforms the paper version, and Hunyuan3D-Part calls the public X-Part a light version. [PartField](https://github.com/nv-tlabs/PartField) [PartSLIP++](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03015) [Hunyuan3D-Part](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-Part)

### Inferences
- **Lowest-friction prototype hypothesis:** Find3D is the cleanest permissive feed-forward text scorer; pair it with an existing/frozen candidate source rather than treating its thresholded semantic heatmap as the final mask. [Find3D repository](https://github.com/ziqi-ma/Find3D)
- **Highest-value restricted prototype hypothesis:** P3-SAM best matches G1’s need for native 3D multi-mask proposals and source-face transfer, but its territorial restriction makes it unusable in South Korea under the published license; the current machine/user context therefore requires a different implementation or separate authorization, not silent adoption. [P3-SAM license](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-Part/main/LICENSE)
- **Research-only option hypothesis:** PartField is attractive for hierarchy ablation but cannot be carried into a commercial pipeline under its published license. [PartField license](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nv-tlabs/PartField/main/LICENSE)
- **Engineering-risk hypothesis:** scene systems are less drop-in than their open licenses suggest because they assume RGB-D sequences, calibrated poses, reconstruction conventions, and sparse-convolution toolchains that a source-mesh asset pipeline may not provide. [OpenMask3D](https://github.com/openmask3d/openmask3d) [Open3DIS](https://github.com/VinAIResearch/Open3DIS) [OpenScene](https://github.com/pengsongyou/openscene)

### Gaps
- This is a technical license inventory, not legal advice; transitive model/dataset licenses, especially SAM-family, GLIP, Grounding-DINO, Objaverse, PartNet, and benchmark assets, need a separate compliance review.
- Exact GPU memory, runtime, and build compatibility on the Edit3D environment were not benchmarked.
- Repository availability can change after the cutoff; OpenPart3D, PS3, and Search3D licensing should be rechecked before implementation.
- No code was executed in this research task, so “available” means officially published, not independently reproduced.
