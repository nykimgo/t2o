# Boundary Diagnosis and Conditional Refinement for Edit3D G3

Research cutoff: 2026-11-12. Scope: training-free or frozen-pretrained methods that can refine a localized target/body boundary on the original source mesh, diagnose likely failure causes, and support bounded E3/E4 actions. This note does not propose changing Module 2 or the frozen mask interface. Diagnostic use of evaluator truth, oracle points, or oracle output choice is explicitly separated from automatic source-only inference. Energies, margins, uncertainty scores, and quality estimates are fallible signals, not correctness guarantees.

## Which classical and learned methods can refine source-mesh faces from RGB, normals, geometry, a coarse mask, or points?

### Takeaway

The most defensible training-free G3 baseline is a local source-face graph whose unary terms come from the coarse mask, prompts, and frozen semantic evidence, and whose pairwise terms use mesh adjacency, RGB, normals, dihedral angle, concavity, and geodesic distance. Binary graph cut, random walker, CRF/Potts smoothing, geodesic contour snapping, neck proposals, and hierarchy/superface snapping offer complementary inductive biases; none can infer a missing distant instance or guarantee that a geometrically plausible seam is the instruction-correct boundary.

### Cited Findings

#### Face-graph optimization

| Method | Source-face formulation and observable objective | Boundary/topology behavior | Main Edit3D limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Binary s/t graph cut** | Treat every source face as a node; hard positive/negative prompts become terminal constraints, coarse-mask or frozen-model scores become unary costs, and adjacent-face RGB/normal/geometry differences become pairwise Potts costs. Boykov and Jolly minimize a region-plus-boundary energy globally for the binary submodular construction, while allowing foreground and background to contain multiple disconnected components. [Original paper](https://www.csd.uwo.ca/~yboykov/Papers/iccv01.pdf) | It relabels existing faces and therefore does not alter mesh connectivity. Hard keep seeds can prevent local body leakage, concavity/dihedral-aware low edge weights can place a seam, and disconnected repeated parts are permitted if each is supported. The boundary-length regularizer can nevertheless erase thin attachments or choose a short body cut when unary evidence is weak. [Graph-cut review](https://www.csd.uwo.ca/~yboykov/Papers/ijcv06.pdf) | The minimum energy is optimal only for the registered energy, not for semantic intent. It cannot discover an omitted distant part if the local domain/unaries contain no evidence for it. [Original paper](https://www.csd.uwo.ca/~yboykov/Papers/iccv01.pdf) |
| **Multi-label graph cut / Potts model** | Alpha-expansion-style optimization can assign faces to target, body, unknown, or candidate-instance labels when the pairwise metric satisfies the method's assumptions; it is useful for retaining an explicit unknown band rather than forcing every face into target/body. [Graph-cut review](https://www.csd.uwo.ca/~yboykov/Papers/ijcv06.pdf) | Multiple labels can preserve neighboring repeated parts and uncertainty bands better than a forced binary cut, but approximation quality and the meaning of every label depend on the energy design. [Graph-cut review](https://www.csd.uwo.ca/~yboykov/Papers/ijcv06.pdf) | An approximate low energy is neither calibrated confidence nor evidence that the candidate pool is sufficient. |
| **Random walker on the face-dual graph** | Seeded faces define absorbing labels; edge conductance can combine RGB, normals, dihedral angle, SDF, and geometry. The method solves a sparse Laplacian system and exposes, for each face, the probability that a walk first reaches each seed. [Grady 2006](https://doi.org/10.1109/TPAMI.2006.233) [Mesh extension](https://rosinpl.github.io/resources/papers/segmentation-SPM.pdf) | Diffusion is robust to noise and supplies a soft boundary band. High conductance along a thin appendage can preserve it, while a low-conductance neck can stop leakage; conversely, a weak or texture-only interface can diffuse into the body. [Mesh extension](https://rosinpl.github.io/resources/papers/segmentation-SPM.pdf) | Hitting probabilities quantify the registered graph and seeds, not semantic correctness. A confidently wrong seed can produce confident wrong probabilities. |
| **CRF / dense CRF / local Potts CRF** | A coarse target probability supplies unaries; Gaussian pairwise terms can couple position, RGB, normals, or frozen features. DenseCRF performs efficient approximate mean-field inference for fully connected Gaussian edge potentials and exposes approximate label marginals. [Krähenbühl and Koltun](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2011/file/beda24c1e1adfa4d4e06f9d74e245a1a-Paper.pdf) [Original code README](https://github.com/lucasb-eyer/pydensecrf/blob/master/pydensecrf/densecrf/README.txt) | Dense appearance coupling can join repeated same-looking parts or propagate across close but geodesically separate surfaces; restricting one term to mesh adjacency and retaining geodesic/normal terms reduces that risk but changes the original dense-image recipe. [DenseCRF code](https://github.com/tvercaut/densecrf) | Mean-field marginals and final energy are approximate and generally uncalibrated. A CRF can sharpen an available boundary but cannot establish that a different candidate or new view is needed. |

#### Geodesic, neck, superface, and hierarchy methods

- **Geodesic contour snapping:** fast geodesic curvature flow starts from a random-walker contour and evolves it toward a nearby feature-weighted local minimum of curve length; its level-set formulation can handle topology changes that a simple snake cannot. This makes it a plausible boundary-only correction after localization, but the result depends on initialization and can snap to the wrong nearby short loop. [Mesh snapping paper](https://jianfei-cai.github.io/Euro10_Geodesic.pdf)
- **Neck/bottleneck proposals:** shape-diameter segmentation estimates local thickness, soft-clusters facets, and uses graph cut with dihedral angle and concavity. CGAL log-normalizes SDF specifically to spread small values and preserve thin parts. Its implementation assumes a closed, oriented, intersection-free, combinatorial 2-manifold for reliable meaning. [CGAL mesh segmentation manual](https://doc.cgal.org/latest/Surface_mesh_segmentation/index.html) [Reference manual](https://doc.cgal.org/latest/Surface_mesh_segmentation/group__PkgSurfaceMeshSegmentationRef.html)
- **Explicit neck loops:** the 2022 neck-feature method uses a volumetric distance-to-boundary Morse function, identifies type-2 saddle critical points, cuts a plane through each candidate, tightens the resulting loop to a closed geodesic, and ranks significance through persistence under smoothing. It covers genus-0 necks as well as handle-related loops, but requires volumetric processing and proposes geometric necks, not instruction semantics. [Fast Computation of Neck-like Features](https://doi.org/10.1109/TVCG.2022.3211781)
- **SDF is not universal:** thickness and concavity are useful for handles, stalks, horns, and other narrow attachments, but a flush, textured, broad, or nearly coplanar attachment can have no SDF minimum. Conversely, a legitimate thin body region can look like a removable neck. The CGAL method is an automatic decomposition method, not a target/body classifier. [CGAL manual](https://doc.cgal.org/latest/Surface_mesh_segmentation/index.html)
- **Variational shape approximation (VSA) superfaces:** VSA iteratively assigns triangles to fitted plane proxies and supports random, incremental, or hierarchical seeding plus custom proxy error metrics. It can reduce a face graph and stabilize noisy normals, but a superface that crosses the true target/body boundary makes that boundary inexpressible unless the algorithm permits splitting or returns to faces. [CGAL VSA reference](https://doc.cgal.org/latest/Surface_mesh_approximation/group__PkgTSMARef.html)
- **Spectral and hierarchical segmentation:** spectral methods construct a face affinity/Laplacian, embed faces using eigenvectors, and cluster them; concavity-aware variants favor cuts at concave seams, while p-spectral clustering can recursively produce a hierarchy. A recent segmentation-field method also reports inner-variation, part-oscillation, and cut scores for candidate boundaries. These scores rank geometric decompositions but are not target confidence. [Foundational spectral mesh segmentation](https://doi.org/10.1109/PCCGA.2004.1348360) [p-spectral hierarchy](https://doi.org/10.1109/TPAMI.2013.2297314) [Segmentation-field method](https://researchportal.hkust.edu.hk/en/publications/spectral-3d-mesh-segmentation-with-a-novel-single-segmentation-fi/)
- **Hierarchy as a boundary-capacity test:** evaluate several registered hierarchy cuts and local parent/child splits before generating new geometry. PartField is a directly relevant frozen learned hierarchy, but its hierarchy score is not an instruction-aware confidence and its public license is research/education-only. [PartField paper and project](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/toronto-ai/partfield-release/) [Official license](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nv-tlabs/PartField/main/LICENSE)

#### Frozen promptable refinement

- **Point-SAM:** the frozen model accepts 3D point-cloud prompts, releases a pretrained ViT-L checkpoint, and demonstrates mesh segmentation by sampling a mesh and propagating point labels back to it. It is a native-3D candidate generator rather than an exact source-face optimizer; sampling density and point-to-face transfer must therefore be evaluated separately on thin triangles and seams. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.17741) [Official repository](https://github.com/zyc00/Point-SAM)
- **P3-SAM:** one positive 3D point yields three alternative masks, and the official automatic path ranks masks by predicted IoU before NMS. Retaining all heads and source-face mappings is useful for E3 candidate retention, but predicted IoU is mask-quality estimation rather than instruction correctness. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.06784) [Official auto-mask code](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-Part/blob/main/P3-SAM/demo/auto_mask.py)
- **SAM/SAM 2 cropped-view refinement:** the official SAM automatic generator exposes predicted IoU, threshold-stability score, crop layers, and point-grid prompting; SAM 2 accepts positive/negative points and mask prompts in its image/video predictor. Registered crops can increase visible boundary resolution, but only depth-visible pixels should vote to source faces and unseen faces must remain unknown until separately supported. [SAM generator](https://github.com/facebookresearch/segment-anything/blob/main/segment_anything/automatic_mask_generator.py) [SAM 2 repository](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam2)
- **Multimask choice remains a separate problem:** SAMRefiner reports that choosing among SAM masks by predicted IoU is better than single-mask mode but still below ground-truth oracle choice; its trainable IoU adaptor is outside the no-task-training constraint, while its observation motivates retaining alternatives and measuring automatic-versus-oracle choice gaps. [SAMRefiner](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.06756)

#### Method behavior on critical failure modes

| Failure mode | Methods that may help | What they preserve, and what can still fail |
|---|---|---|
| Thin attachment omitted or eroded | SDF log-normalization, face-level random walker, positive point prompts, and face rather than coarse-superface optimization. [CGAL reference](https://doc.cgal.org/latest/Surface_mesh_segmentation/group__PkgSurfaceMeshSegmentationRef.html) [Random-walk mesh paper](https://rosinpl.github.io/resources/papers/segmentation-SPM.pdf) | Thinness is a geometric cue, not proof of target identity. Smoothness, minimum-component filters, sparse sampling, and superfaces can erase the target. |
| Body leakage across a junction | Hard background/keep seeds, concavity/dihedral pairwise terms, neck-loop candidates, and local-domain restriction. [Graph cuts](https://www.csd.uwo.ca/~yboykov/Papers/iccv01.pdf) [Neck features](https://doi.org/10.1109/TVCG.2022.3211781) | A broad or smooth junction may provide no geometric barrier; too-small local domains can truncate the true target. |
| Repeated/bilateral parts | Disconnected graph-cut foreground, separate positive seeds per requested instance, native 3D prompt candidates, and hierarchy alternatives. [Graph cuts](https://www.csd.uwo.ca/~yboykov/Papers/iccv01.pdf) [Point-SAM](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.17741) | Local refinement cannot recover an omitted distant counterpart unless localization or reselection supplies it. Dense appearance coupling can merge similar instances. |
| Topology change in the label boundary | Binary graph cut and level-set geodesic curvature flow allow disconnected components or contour topology changes. [Graph cuts](https://www.csd.uwo.ca/~yboykov/Papers/iccv01.pdf) [Mesh snapping](https://jianfei-cai.github.io/Euro10_Geodesic.pdf) | Label topology freedom is not semantic topology preservation; unrestricted cuts can create islands or sever the body. |
| No candidate expresses the seam | Face-level graph optimization, new point prompts, cropped SAM 2 masks, or a finer hierarchy can add capacity. [Point-SAM](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.17741) [SAM generator](https://github.com/facebookresearch/segment-anything/blob/main/segment_anything/automatic_mask_generator.py) | Failure of one finite method/menu proves only insufficiency of that registered menu, not impossibility of refinement. |

### Inferences

- **Proposal — local face-graph baseline:** construct a fixed-width geodesic band around the coarse-mask boundary; freeze confident target interior and confident exterior/keep faces; optimize only the band. Use area-weighted unaries from coarse-mask logits and frozen semantic evidence, and pairwise features from edge length, RGB difference, normal angle, signed concavity, and SDF difference. This is an Edit3D proposal synthesized from seeded graph cuts and SDF segmentation, not a published guarantee. [Graph cuts](https://www.csd.uwo.ca/~yboykov/Papers/iccv01.pdf) [CGAL SDF segmentation](https://doc.cgal.org/latest/Surface_mesh_segmentation/index.html)
- **Proposal — preserve expressivity:** run the same registered energy at source-face resolution and on superfaces. A superface result is eligible only when every boundary superface can be split back to faces; otherwise report `candidate_boundary_insufficient_at_this_granularity`. VSA's proxy errors can prioritize splits, but not certify semantic boundaries. [CGAL VSA](https://doc.cgal.org/latest/Surface_mesh_approximation/group__PkgTSMARef.html)
- **Proposal — topology and repetition guards:** do not impose one connected foreground component. Require preservation of each localized requested instance, report added/removed connected components, and forbid a refinement domain around one component from being interpreted as evidence about an omitted distant instance. Binary graph cuts permit disconnected topology, but instance completeness remains an external instruction constraint. [Graph cuts](https://www.csd.uwo.ca/~yboykov/Papers/iccv01.pdf)
- **Proposal — neck cues as candidates, not rules:** add neck/SDF/geodesic loops to the candidate boundary set and score them under the same frozen objective; never automatically declare the narrowest loop to be the removable seam. Neck methods identify geometry, not target semantics. [Neck features](https://doi.org/10.1109/TVCG.2022.3211781)

### Gaps

- No reviewed method jointly guarantees free-form instruction identity, all-instance completeness, thin-part preservation, and zero body leakage on arbitrary textured source meshes.
- The literature does not establish a universal weighting among RGB, normal, concavity, SDF, semantic, and smoothness terms; Edit3D must freeze these on calibration cases and report sensitivity.
- Most classical mesh papers assume clean manifold meshes. Reliability on open, self-intersecting, non-manifold, or disconnected production assets remains an empirical question. [CGAL input assumptions](https://doc.cgal.org/latest/Surface_mesh_segmentation/index.html)
- Point-cloud prompt methods do not establish lossless source-face transfer, especially on tiny triangles, hidden surfaces, and thin structures. [Point-SAM](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.17741)

## Which objectives and confidence signals can diagnose selection, observation, or candidate-boundary insufficiency and support bounded actions?

### Takeaway

Useful diagnostics are plural and conditional: alternative-candidate evidence supports RESELECT; visibility and cross-view evidence support OBSERVE; localized semantic agreement plus boundary instability supports REFINE; calibrated acceptance risk can support ACCEPT or UNRESOLVED. No energy, entropy, predicted IoU, or VLM verdict alone identifies the true failure cause, and automatic inference cannot reproduce evaluator-only finite-pool sufficiency proofs.

### Cited Findings

#### Observable signals and their limits

| Signal | What it measures | Potential E4 use | Why it is not a guarantee |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Graph-cut energy and constrained energy margin** | Fit to the registered unary/pairwise objective; binary s/t min-cut is globally optimal for the constructed submodular energy. [Boykov and Jolly](https://www.csd.uwo.ca/~yboykov/Papers/iccv01.pdf) | Compare the current cut with registered alternatives or constrained re-solves; a small margin can trigger REFINE/UNRESOLVED. | Low energy can encode a wrong semantic prior or over-smooth a thin target. The raw scale changes with graph size and weights. |
| **Random-walker probabilities, entropy, and boundary margin** | Harmonic hitting probabilities induced by seeds and graph conductance; full per-label probabilities are directly available in implementations. [scikit-image API](https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.segmentation.html) | Localize ambiguous boundary faces and test sensitivity to seed placement. | Probabilities are conditional on possibly wrong seeds/features and are not calibrated probabilities of mask correctness. |
| **CRF marginals/entropy and parameter sensitivity** | Approximate mean-field beliefs under unary and Gaussian pairwise terms. [DenseCRF paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2011/file/beda24c1e1adfa4d4e06f9d74e245a1a-Paper.pdf) | Boundary uncertainty map; compare adjacency-only and dense appearance terms. | Approximate marginals can be sharply wrong, and dense same-appearance coupling can leak between separate surfaces. |
| **Hierarchy stability, eigen/cut scores, and cross-level agreement** | Stability of geometric partitions over hierarchy levels or perturbations; spectral methods expose eigenvectors and cut criteria. [p-spectral clustering](https://doi.org/10.1109/TPAMI.2013.2297314) [Segmentation field](https://researchportal.hkust.edu.hk/en/publications/spectral-3d-mesh-segmentation-with-a-novel-single-segmentation-fi/) | If several levels agree on identity but disagree near one seam, consider REFINE; if an alternative level cleanly supplies a missing part, consider RESELECT. | Spectral confidence is geometric partition confidence, not instruction or body-safety confidence. |
| **SAM-family predicted IoU and stability score** | The model's own quality prediction and mask stability under binarization-threshold changes; the official generator filters on both. [SAM generator](https://github.com/facebookresearch/segment-anything/blob/main/segment_anything/automatic_mask_generator.py) | Rank prompt/crop alternatives and flag unstable boundaries. | Predicted IoU can select the wrong alternative; SAMRefiner measures a gap to ground-truth oracle selection. [SAMRefiner](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.06756) |
| **Ensemble or perturbation disagreement** | Pairwise overlap among predictions from heads, view subsets, prompts, crops, or parameter perturbations. A multi-dataset medical benchmark found ensemble pairwise Dice the strongest tested failure-detection baseline and recommends risk-coverage/AURC. [Failure-detection benchmark](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.03323) | High disagreement with adequate visibility can route to RESELECT/REFINE; disagreement concentrated in unseen faces can route to OBSERVE. | Agreement can be confidently wrong, especially when models share training data, architecture, views, or semantic errors. Cross-domain medical results motivate a baseline, not an Edit3D guarantee. |
| **Learned quality regression / error map** | Predicts expected overlap or spatial errors from an image and mask; SegQC is an example that trains a separate voxel-error network. [SegQC](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.07601) | Research comparator for automatic localization and quality estimation. | It requires task/domain training and is therefore ineligible for the no-task-specific-training E3/E4 protocol; reported medical performance does not transfer automatically. |
| **Selective risk and coverage** | Error among accepted predictions versus accepted fraction. Conformal risk control can control expected monotone losses under its calibration/exchangeability assumptions. [Conformal Risk Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.02814) [Learn then Test](https://doi.org/10.1214/24-AOAS1998) | Calibrate an ACCEPT threshold and expose UNRESOLVED rather than silently passing low-confidence masks. | Guarantees require a held-out exchangeable calibration regime and a specified loss; development reuse or distribution shift invalidates a guarantee. |

- **Calibration metrics must be local and class-aware:** ECE can be dominated by high-confidence background faces and fixed-bin choices; calibration work recommends alternatives such as ACE/TACE and proper scoring rules, while local-calibration research notes that aggregate ECE does not establish reliability of an individual prediction. [Calibration measurement](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPRW_2019/papers/Uncertainty%20and%20Robustness%20in%20Deep%20Visual%20Learning/Nixon_Measuring_Calibration_in_Deep_Learning_CVPRW_2019_paper.pdf) [Local calibration](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v180/luo22a/luo22a.pdf)
- **Segmentation failure evaluation should include abstention:** risk-coverage curves and AURC jointly show the quality of accepted outputs and the fraction withheld; reporting accuracy only among accepted masks can reward a controller that abstains on nearly everything. [Failure-detection benchmark](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.03323)

#### Method-to-failure and action mapping

The following is a **proposal for Edit3D**, not a claim that the signals uniquely identify causes.

| Hypothesized failure | Diagnostic-oracle localization | Automatic source-only evidence to test | Bounded action | Disconfirming evidence / safe fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Selection error**: current mask is wrong, but a registered alternative is adequate | Evaluator truth shows a P0/P1/P2 alternative or feasible union meets the target/body criterion. This is a finite-pool oracle result, not runtime input. | A registered alternative improves instruction entity/count support, multiview support, and body-risk features by a frozen margin; current boundary energy need not be high. | **RESELECT** over the full registered pool. | If no automatic margin is reliable, choose **UNRESOLVED**. Do not infer pool insufficiency from failure to select. |
| **Observation insufficiency**: potentially adequate hypothesis, insufficient visible evidence | Evaluator identifies target/boundary faces that were unseen or weakly observed; an added registered view resolves them. | Low visible-face count or projected area, depth occlusion near the disputed seam, and view-subset disagreement that a candidate camera is predicted to reduce. | **OBSERVE**, within the fixed view budget, then rerun the same selector. | If adequate clear views already agree, more views are not justified; route to RESELECT, REFINE, or UNRESOLVED. |
| **Candidate-boundary insufficiency**: identity/location is right, no checked candidate fits the seam | E1 exact finite-pool infeasibility or E3 diagnostic point/region plus evaluator best-choice fails for every registered candidate. The conclusion is limited to that finite pool/menu. | Strong entity/count support and adequate visibility, but every checked candidate has consistent local spill/omission, hierarchy/superface straddling, or unstable seam under registered perturbations. | **REFINE** once with a validated face-graph, point, or crop method. | Automatic evidence cannot prove exhaustive insufficiency. If localization is weak or the omitted target is distant, use RESELECT/OBSERVE or UNRESOLVED. |
| **Correct mask / no supported defect** | Evaluator confirms the mask; used only to measure false alarms and good-mask regression. | Entity/count checks pass, visibility is adequate, boundary and alternative tests show no supported defect, and acceptance score passes a frozen calibration threshold. | **ACCEPT**. | Confidence is fallible. If evidence conflicts or calibration coverage is unsupported, use **UNRESOLVED**. |
| **Mixed or unresolved failure** | Multiple actions help, no action helps, truth is uncertain, or finite search times out. | Conflicting semantic, visibility, and boundary signals; action budget exhausted; diagnostic schema incomplete. | **UNRESOLVED**. | Never convert unresolved into empty-mask success or automatic Module 2 execution. |

#### Diagnostic oracle versus automatic localization

- `diagnostic_location` may use one evaluator-supplied interior point per requested instance and a coarse source-face region, but not the true boundary; it measures whether a correction method can work when location is known.
- `diagnostic_mask_choice` may additionally choose the best generated mask against evaluator truth; it is an upper bound on finite generated alternatives, not a deployable selector.
- `automatic_location_and_choice` must use only source mesh, instruction, source-derived renders/features, and registered predictions. Its gap to the two diagnostic conditions separates action feasibility from localization/selection quality.
- A failed diagnostic action shows that the registered method/menu did not solve that case. It does not prove that no classical or learned method could solve it.

### Inferences

- **Proposal — separate three confidence layers:** record (1) semantic/instance confidence, (2) observation sufficiency, and (3) boundary/candidate adequacy. Do not average them before action selection; a low combined score cannot distinguish RESELECT, OBSERVE, and REFINE.
- **Proposal — energy margin by intervention:** for graph methods, recompute the best energy under registered interventions such as forcing a disputed component target/body, changing only the seed subset, or restricting the cut to each neck/hierarchy proposal. Normalize by boundary-band area or edge measure and calibrate margins empirically. This yields an auditable sensitivity measure, not probability of correctness.
- **Proposal — confidence localization:** report facewise uncertainty only where direct source-face correspondence and visibility are known. View-space uncertainty on occluded pixels must not be projected through the object or used to label unseen back faces.
- **Proposal — selective controller:** tune ACCEPT/UNRESOLVED thresholds on a frozen calibration split and report the full risk-coverage curve. Given the small, development-influenced Edit3D evidence, describe thresholds as empirical operating points unless a genuinely held-out exchangeable calibration protocol exists. [Conformal Risk Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.02814)

### Gaps

- No reviewed scalar confidence reliably distinguishes semantic selection error from geometric boundary error.
- No source establishes that two calls to the same VLM or two heads of the same segmenter are independent uncertainty evidence.
- Edit3D currently lacks enough untouched calibration data to claim conformal or selective-risk guarantees; empirical calibration and external confirmation remain necessary.
- Automatic candidate-pool insufficiency is fundamentally harder than detecting a bad current mask: runtime cannot inspect evaluator truth or exhaust every possible future candidate.

## What controlled E3/E4 baselines, ablations, metrics, and official implementations should Edit3D use?

### Takeaway

E3 should compare correction families under identical diagnostic points/regions, local domains, feature budgets, and source-face evaluation, including already-good controls. E4 should compare no correction, always-correct, fixed retry, diagnosis-driven routing, and a finite evaluator-only best-action oracle at the same budget. Primary reporting must separate target recall, body contamination/damage, per-instance completeness, boundary quality, good-mask regression, action harm, abstention, and cost.

#### Proposed ablations

- **A1 — evidence channels:** coarse-mask unary only; `+RGB`; `+normals/dihedral`; `+concavity/SDF`; `+frozen semantic score`. Add one channel at a time and keep normalization fixed.
- **A2 — graph support:** source-face adjacency; superface graph; adjacency plus one dense appearance term. Report whether the candidate boundary is expressible before comparing final quality.
- **A3 — local domain:** two or more preregistered geodesic-band widths or the protocol's fixed crop extents. Report true-target truncation in the diagnostic condition; do not tune width per case.
- **A4 — prompts:** current interior seed only; one evaluator interior point per requested instance; automatic points; positive plus registered negative points where the API supports them. P3-SAM negative points must not be assumed if its unchanged API does not expose them.
- **A5 — boundary proposals:** no snapping; dihedral/concavity minima; SDF minima; explicit neck loops; hierarchy boundaries. This tests cues, not semantic rules.
- **A6 — confidence:** raw energy/entropy/predicted IoU; perturbation disagreement; calibrated combined score. Evaluate each against actual mask/action outcomes, not only correlation.
- **A7 — oracle separation:** diagnostic location; diagnostic location plus evaluator best-choice; automatic location and choice. Never pool these scores.

#### Good-mask regression and negative controls

- Include masks that already satisfy strict target/body criteria, not only attractive failures. Report any new target omission, body inclusion, connected-component change, or severe human-rated damage after every refiner.
- Compare **always apply** each eligible refiner with a diagnosis-gated version. A method that recovers failures but damages good masks is not safe merely because mean IoU rises.
- Use evaluator-only synthetic perturbations as controlled diagnostics: boundary dilation into body, erosion from target, one omitted repeated component, one added wrong component, and a displaced local region. These perturbations test whether diagnostics distinguish boundary, selection, and location failures; they are not production inputs.
- Include a wrong-localization control: apply the same bounded refiner around a non-target or wrong selected component. This measures whether the method obediently sharpens the wrong entity.
- Freeze all thresholds and parameter grids on calibration cases, then publish every check-case output. Do not choose a per-case winning energy, hierarchy level, crop, or prompt count.

#### Metrics

- **Primary source-surface mask metrics:** physical-area target recall `R`, selected-area contamination `C`, protected-area damage `D`, IoU, uncertain-area selection, per-requested-instance recall, and entirely missed instances. These retain asymmetric target/body safety rather than allowing IoU to hide leakage.
- **Boundary metrics:** length-weighted boundary precision and recall with a preregistered geodesic tolerance, boundary F-score, symmetric mean geodesic boundary distance, and a robust high percentile such as HD95. Mesh-segmentation literature uses cut discrepancy and boundary precision/recall, but cut discrepancy has degeneracies under oversegmentation, so it should not stand alone. [Metric comparison](https://perso.liris.cnrs.fr/guillaume.lavoue/revue/VC2010.pdf) [Boundary precision/recall definitions](https://3d.bk.tudelft.nl/weixiao/assets/files/paper_pssnet.pdf)
- **Thin/repeated/topology strata:** target recall by local thickness quantile; boundary scores at necks versus non-necks; requested-instance count recall; connected target/body component changes; holes/islands introduced; and the fraction of true boundary crossing a superface.
- **Paired correction metrics:** delta R/C/D/IoU/boundary F per case, strict failures recovered, previously good masks regressed, severe body-damage count, harmful-action rate, and action cost. Report gains and regressions separately rather than only their net mean.
- **Diagnostic metrics:** one-vs-rest and per-cause precision/recall, AUROC and AUPRC under class imbalance, confusion among selection/observation/boundary/other, localization overlap with actual error faces, and action-outcome utility. Several actions may help one case, so also report whether the chosen action helped relative to no action and regret to the finite same-budget oracle.
- **Selective-prediction metrics:** risk-coverage curves, AURC, acceptance coverage at registered risk targets, false-accept rate, false-unresolved rate, and accuracy among accepted outputs. AURC is recommended in segmentation failure-detection benchmarking. [Failure-detection benchmark](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.03323)
- **Calibration metrics:** reliability diagrams and Brier/log loss for each defined event, plus adaptive/thresholded calibration error rather than ECE alone. Report separate events for strict mask failure, severe body leakage, and each action's harmful outcome. [Calibration measurement](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPRW_2019/papers/Uncertainty%20and%20Robustness%20in%20Deep%20Visual%20Learning/Nixon_Measuring_Calibration_in_Deep_Learning_CVPRW_2019_paper.pdf)
- **Cost metrics:** wall time, peak memory, number of rendered views, prompts, graph solves, VLM/model calls, and fraction exhausting the two-round action budget.
- **Statistical reporting:** paired per-asset results, asset-level bootstrap intervals, explicit unresolved denominators, and separate diagnostic-oracle versus automatic results. Aggregate face counts must not be treated as independent samples.

#### Proposed E4 comparisons

1. Frozen E2 output with **no correction**.
2. **Always apply** each E3-eligible correction separately.
3. A **fixed retry** policy with the same action/call budget.
4. The proposed **diagnosis-driven** `ACCEPT / RESELECT / OBSERVE / REFINE / UNRESOLVED` controller.
5. An **evaluator-only finite best-action oracle** over exactly the same menu and budget.

The finite oracle measures headroom of the registered menu. It does not represent an achievable automatic judge and does not prove that cases outside the menu are unsolvable.

### Inferences

- **Proposal — minimum practical E3 set:** start with G3-0, face-level graph cut, random walker, one hierarchy/superface arm, frozen native-3D prompting, and cropped SAM 2 lifting. This spans exact discrete optimization, soft graph uncertainty, existing granularity, new 3D candidates, and higher-resolution 2D evidence without task-specific training.
- **Proposal — promotion rule:** an E3 action becomes E4-eligible only after it shows recoveries under diagnostic location, reports automatic-location loss, measures regressions on good masks, and freezes cost/parameter bounds. Mean IoU improvement alone is insufficient.
- **Proposal — safe licensing path:** use libigl for SDF/geodesic features, an independently audited max-flow implementation for the proposed face graph, Point-SAM for permissive 3D prompting, and SAM 2 for 2D crop ablation. Treat CGAL's mesh segmentation as GPL/commercial and PartField/P3-SAM as restricted research comparisons.
- **Proposal — interface preservation:** keep every experiment upstream of the frozen mask contract. Emit the refined source-face mask through the existing conversion path and store energies, probabilities, visibility, diagnosis, action, and unresolved status only in orchestration/evaluation sidecars.

### Gaps

- License notes are a technical inventory, not legal advice; checkpoint, dataset, patent, and transitive dependency terms require separate review.
- No code was executed in this literature task, so repository availability does not establish buildability, runtime, or numerical reproducibility in the Edit3D environment.
- There is no accepted universal boundary tolerance for Edit3D; it must be set in physical/source-mesh units before comparison and accompanied by tolerance sensitivity.
- The ten confirmed-patch cases are valuable diagnostics but are insufficient by themselves to establish general calibration, selective-risk guarantees, or unseen-asset safety.
