# 3D Part Removal and Surface Restoration Literature

Research cutoff: 2026-11-12. This note covers verifiable work available by that date. It treats Module 2 and the existing `MaskInterfaceContract` as frozen. Restoration methods are prior-art context and deferred comparison candidates, not proposals to alter the current masking work.

## Which papers directly remove a selected attachment and reconstruct the exposed body surface versus regenerate the whole asset?

### Takeaway

No reviewed paper cleanly solves the full Edit3D problem as one operation: accept an instruction-selected attachment on an arbitrary source mesh, delete exactly those source faces, reconstruct the newly exposed body surface, and leave every unselected source face and index unchanged. Classical methods are genuinely local and can preserve the source outside a supplied hole, but they do not identify semantic attachments or know the hidden body shape; recent semantic systems can perform deletion, but usually invert and resynthesize a neural, voxel, NeRF, Gaussian, or diffusion representation rather than preserve the original mesh exactly.

### Cited Findings

#### Claim-evidence matrix

| **Liepa, Filling Holes in Meshes (SGP 2003)** | Given a boundary loop in an oriented connected manifold triangle mesh, triangulates the hole, refines the patch to surrounding density, and fairs it. | **Only after another process deletes faces.** It fills the resulting loop; it neither selects a semantic part nor infers whether a smooth patch is the formerly hidden body. | Strong local baseline: the patch is added at the hole rather than regenerating the whole asset. | **Poisson Surface Reconstruction (SGP 2006)** | Reconstructs a global implicit indicator function from oriented points by solving a Poisson problem and extracting an isosurface. | **No.** It reconstructs a complete surface from samples; it is not a local attachment-deletion operator. | Robust closure comes at the cost of global resampling, so exact source triangles, UVs, sharp boundaries, and unchanged-face identity are not guaranteed. | [Paper](https://hhoppe.com/poissonrecon.pdf); [official implementation](https://github.com/mkazhdan/PoissonRecon) |
| **MeshFix (Attene 2010)** | Repairs defects, removes self-intersections and degeneracies, and outputs one watertight triangle mesh for a single closed solid while attempting to leave non-defective regions unmodified. | **No semantic deletion.** It is useful after a cut but is designed for repair of digitized meshes, not for reconstructing the body under a deliberately removed attachment. | More preservation-oriented than global learned completion, but its repair decisions can be coarse on inputs outside its assumptions. | [Official repository and algorithm note](https://github.com/MarcoAttene/MeshFix-V2.1); [paper DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00371-010-0416-3) |
| **Context-Based Coherent Surface Completion (TOG 2014)** | Synthesizes a missing patch so every local neighborhood in the completion resembles a neighborhood on the remaining source surface; it minimizes a multiscale coherence objective. | **No semantic deletion, but closer to natural feature continuation.** It can reconstruct a supplied large hole using source exemplars rather than only minimum curvature. | It operates on the target hole while drawing geometric examples from the source, but cannot recover a unique hidden surface when no suitable exemplar exists. | [ACM paper page](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2532548); [author-hosted paper](https://www.ee.technion.ac.il/~ayellet/Ps/13-HTG.pdf) |
| **Feature-aware variational completion (Bergmann et al. 2023)** | Uses a unified variational framework for denoising, hole filling, and context-aware completion, with an edge mask controlling smooth, sharp, or blended joining. | **No semantic deletion.** It is a local geometric continuation method once a damaged region and optional template are supplied. | More explicit than isotropic fairing about crease preservation, but still needs a valid boundary/template and does not know the instruction-defined target. | [Journal article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10851-022-01107-w) |
| **Laplacian/Poisson mesh editing** | Deforms selected mesh regions while satisfying positional or gradient-field constraints and retaining detail relative to differential coordinates. | **Not hole filling by itself.** It edits existing connectivity; it does not invent the missing body after faces are removed. | Excellent for anchored local deformation, but preservation depends on handles, support region, and constraints rather than an immutable-copy guarantee. | [Laplacian Surface Editing](https://igl.ethz.ch/projects/Laplacian-mesh-processing/Laplacian-mesh-editing/laplacian-mesh-editing.pdf); [Poisson Mesh Editing](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/PoissonMeshEditing.pdf) |
| **DeepSDF (CVPR 2019)** | Fits a category-level continuous signed-distance decoder and infers a complete shape from partial or noisy SDF observations. | **No.** It decodes a complete category-prior shape from a latent code rather than patching an exact source mesh. | Watertight implicit output is possible, but latent fitting and Marching Cubes replace source geometry and can project unusual assets toward learned prototypes. | [CVPR paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/papers/Park_DeepSDF_Learning_Continuous_Signed_Distance_Functions_for_Shape_Representation_CVPR_2019_paper.pdf); [official code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/DeepSDF) |
| **IF-Nets (CVPR 2020)** | Completes shapes from sparse/dense point clouds or voxels using spatial multiscale features, improving retention of observed detail over global-latent implicit models. | **No.** It predicts a complete occupancy surface and remeshes it; there is no semantic attachment-removal operation. | Better observation fidelity than earlier implicit models, but still reconstructs the full surface and explicitly fails when an input lies too far outside its training distribution. | [CVPR paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/papers/Chibane_Implicit_Functions_in_Feature_Space_for_3D_Shape_Reconstruction_and_CVPR_2020_paper.pdf); [project/code](https://virtualhumans.mpi-inf.mpg.de/ifnets/) |
| **ShapeFormer (CVPR 2022)** | Samples diverse complete shapes conditioned on an incomplete point cloud through a sparse vector-quantized implicit representation and transformer. | **No.** Its objective is plausible whole-shape completion under ambiguity. | Diversity is useful when the missing geometry is unknowable, but a plausible sample is not exact source preservation or evidence of the true exposed body. | [CVPR paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Yan_ShapeFormer_Transformer-Based_Shape_Completion_via_Sparse_Representation_CVPR_2022_paper.html) |
| **SPAGHETTI (TOG 2022)** | Learns a part-aware implicit generative representation supporting part transformation, mixing, replacement, and removal; unseen assets require shape inversion before editing. | **Deletion-capable, but not surgical source-mesh repair.** Removing a latent part and recomposing occupancy can close geometry through the learned prior. | The entire output is a decoded implicit shape; inversion error exists before any edit and original triangles/UVs are not retained exactly. | [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.13168); [official project](https://igl.ethz.ch/projects/SPAGHETTI/); [code, pretrained models, and UI](https://github.com/amirhertz/spaghetti) |
| **ParSEL (2024 preprint)** | Converts language plus a segmented mesh into a parameterized analytic edit program and propagates compatible geometric operations. | **No demonstrated exposed-surface reconstruction.** It is strong for controlled transformations of segmented parts, not deletion followed by body inpainting. | Analytic operations can preserve untouched mesh regions more directly than diffusion, but the method presupposes segmentation and an edit expressible by its operation library. | [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.20319) |
| **NeRF/3DGS text editors** — Instruct-NeRF2NeRF, Vox-E, GaussianEditor, GS-Edit | Edit rendered views or optimize radiance fields/Gaussians under text/image diffusion guidance; some localize edits spatially. | **Not an exact mesh-removal solution.** They may visually erase an object or alter density/Gaussians, but do not necessarily output a watertight, manifold source mesh with a repaired exposed surface. | Appearance consistency can be good while topology, source-face identity, and exact geometry remain undefined or require a separate meshing/reconstruction step. | [Instruct-NeRF2NeRF, ICCV 2023](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/html/Haque_Instruct-NeRF2NeRF_Editing_3D_Scenes_with_Instructions_ICCV_2023_paper.html); [Vox-E, ICCV 2023](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/html/Sella_Vox-E_Text-Guided_Voxel_Editing_of_3D_Objects_ICCV_2023_paper.html); [GaussianEditor, CVPR 2024](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Chen_GaussianEditor_Swift_and_Controllable_3D_Editing_with_Gaussian_Splatting_CVPR_2024_paper.html); [GS-Edit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.05154) |
| **PartGen (CVPR 2025)** | Segments a monolithic object into multiview-consistent parts, completes each occluded part with diffusion, then reconstructs separate 3D parts. | **No.** It reconstructs editable parts and their hidden sides; it does not remove one attachment and restore the body beneath it. | The part-completion stage deliberately generates plausible hidden part geometry and reconstructs outputs, so exact source mesh preservation is not its objective. | [CVPR paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/Chen_PartGen_Part-level_3D_Generation_and_Reconstruction_with_Multi-view_Diffusion_Models_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf) |
| **SPLICE (2025 preprint)** | Encodes parts independently in a neural implicit representation and supports translation, rotation, scaling, duplication, mixing, and deletion. | **Deletion-capable, but generative recomposition.** The output is a newly decoded global implicit field rather than the original mesh plus a local patch. | Part isolation reduces spill between latent components, but does not establish vertex-wise identity or preservation of the source outside the selected part. | [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.04514) |
| **Vinedresser3D (CVPR 2026)** | Uses an MLLM, PartField, image editing, and TRELLIS latent inpainting for addition, modification, and deletion; deletion marks target occupied voxels as editable. | **Closest semantic deletion system reviewed, but still not an explicit exposed-body continuation method.** It performs 3D latent inpainting and decodes a new representation; it does not formulate attachment-boundary continuation on the original source mesh. | It measures unedited-region similarity and can use a human or automatic mask, but no exact preservation of source triangles, topology, or UVs is claimed. | [CVPR paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Chi_Vinedresser3D_Towards_Agentic_Text-guided_3D_Editing_CVPR_2026_paper.html); [project](https://vinedresser3d.github.io/); [paper HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.19542) |
| **PartRAG (2026 preprint)** | Freezes non-target part latents and resynthesizes selected part latents for swaps, attribute refinement, and composition. | **No demonstrated deletion-plus-body restoration.** It edits a generated/reconstructed part representation, not an original source-face mask. | Freezing non-target latents constrains the generator, but is not equivalent to preserving source vertices or faces exactly. | [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17033); [claimed code repository](https://github.com/AIGeeksGroup/PartRAG) |
| **CompoSE (2026 preprint)** | Generates part-separated shapes from user-controlled boxes and text, then edits parts while optionally freezing other part latents. | **No.** It is compositional synthesis and part editing, not surgical editing of an arbitrary existing source mesh. | “Exact” preservation in the paper refers to frozen generated part identity in latent space, not exact retention of an input asset's triangles or boundary parameterization. | [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19350) |

The classical survey literature explicitly separates mesh-, surface-, and volume-based hole filling and observes that no one method handles every hole type. It also states that a robust repair method should produce a watertight, non-self-intersecting mesh while avoiding approximation or resampling of measured source data. [Guo et al. survey](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00371-016-1316-y); [comparison of 34 methods over 19 properties](https://doi.org/10.1515/amcs-2016-0063)

### Inferences

- The closest decomposition of the Edit3D task is therefore **semantic source-face selection followed by constrained local completion**, not whole-shape completion. Liepa/PDE/context-based methods address the second half only after a reliable cut boundary exists, while Vinedresser3D/SPAGHETTI address semantic or part-level deletion only through a generative representation. [Liepa](https://diglib.eg.org/items/bd5c3658-4de6-4336-a7bd-0baf4f5618ac); [Vinedresser3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.19542); [SPAGHETTI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.13168)
- “Part-aware” should not be treated as synonymous with “source-preserving.” SPAGHETTI, SPLICE, PartRAG, and CompoSE isolate latent parts, but all decode generated geometry rather than preserve a source mesh by construction. [SPAGHETTI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.13168); [SPLICE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.04514); [PartRAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17033); [CompoSE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19350)
- A watertight cap is not necessarily a natural continuation. Smooth fairing minimizes an energy; context-based completion copies or adapts source features; a learned completion samples a prior. These are different claims and need separate evaluation. [Xu et al.](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~sequin/CS284/PAPERS/Xu_DiscreteSurfaceModPDE.pdf); [Harary et al.](https://www.ee.technion.ac.il/~ayellet/Ps/13-HTG.pdf); [ShapeFormer](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Yan_ShapeFormer_Transformer-Based_Shape_Completion_via_Sparse_Representation_CVPR_2022_paper.html)

### Gaps

- No reviewed benchmark supplies paired arbitrary meshes with the same object both before attachment and after physically correct attachment removal, plus exact source-face correspondences for unchanged-region evaluation.
- No reviewed method demonstrates all of the following together: instruction-conditioned multi-instance deletion, exact source-face retention outside a supplied mask, watertight local topology, feature-aware body continuation, and preservation of UV/material/rig data.
- Vinedresser3D demonstrates deletion examples, but its paper does not isolate an “attachment removed from a fused body” benchmark or evaluate the recovered hidden body against known ground-truth geometry. [Vinedresser3D paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.19542)

## How do methods quantify unchanged-region preservation, watertightness, topology, sharp-feature preservation, and natural continuation?

### Takeaway

The evaluation traditions are fragmented. Classical repair emphasizes boundary closure, manifoldness, self-intersection, continuity, and local geometric error; learned completion emphasizes global Chamfer distance, IoU, F-score, or normal consistency; text-guided editing adds render-space similarity and human preference. Very few papers jointly report exact unchanged-source error, topology, and perceptual naturalness, so a visually plausible result can still drift geometrically or remain unsuitable as an editable mesh.

### Cited Findings

| Property | Metrics or evidence actually used in prior work | What the metric proves | What it does not prove | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Unchanged-region preservation** | Vinedresser3D reports Chamfer distance plus PSNR, SSIM, and LPIPS on unedited regions. Its automatic-mask result is CD 0.016, PSNR 29.45, SSIM 0.953, LPIPS 0.045; the human-mask variant reports 0.008, 37.69, 0.984, and 0.015. | Measures geometric/render similarity on the authors' benchmark and shows the cost of imperfect automatic localization. | Does not prove vertex identity, zero source-face displacement, UV/material preservation, or unchanged topology. | [Vinedresser3D evaluation table](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.19542) |
| **Preservation by construction** | Local surface fillers modify or create the patch bounded by the hole; MeshFix states that regions without defects are left unmodified. | Stronger evidence than a global similarity score when the implementation truly copies unaffected source data. | Does not show that the hole boundary corresponds to the correct semantic attachment or that the patch is the true hidden body. | [Liepa](https://diglib.eg.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/f7b15c83-bc9f-4286-9ec5-c81a02143f34/content); [MeshFix README](https://github.com/MarcoAttene/MeshFix-V2.1/blob/master/README.txt) |
| **Watertightness and manifold topology** | Classical criteria count open boundary loops/edges and inspect non-manifold or self-intersecting output. Volumetric diffusion states a manifold, non-interpenetrating guarantee; MeshFix targets one watertight triangle mesh bounding a polyhedron. | Establishes closure and basic mesh validity under each method's assumptions. | A closed surface may contain a dent, flat plug, wrong genus, or implausible body continuation. | [Davis et al.](https://graphics.stanford.edu/papers/holefill-3dpvt02/); [MeshFix](https://github.com/MarcoAttene/MeshFix-V2.1); [hole-filling comparison](https://doi.org/10.1515/amcs-2016-0063) |
| **Boundary smoothness/continuity** | PDE methods impose or evaluate positional, tangent, curvature, or higher-order continuity; Xu et al. explicitly study second-, fourth-, and sixth-order flows with specified geometric continuity. | Shows how smoothly the new patch joins the old surface. | High-order smoothness can erase intended creases and cannot identify the correct hidden geometry. | [Xu et al.](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~sequin/CS284/PAPERS/Xu_DiscreteSurfaceModPDE.pdf) |
| **Sharp-feature preservation** | Feature-aware methods use crease/edge masks, curvature terms, or sharpness-dependent filtering; the 2023 unified framework permits smooth, sharp, or blended joining by changing an edge mask. | Tests or enforces whether a known feature should continue through the fill. | Requires reliable feature evidence or a template; a missing feature cannot be uniquely recovered from a smooth rim. | [Feature-aware framework](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10851-022-01107-w); [Context-based completion references and comparison](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2532548) |
| **Natural or context-aware continuation** | Harary et al. define coherence error as the average over target patches of the minimum dissimilarity to a source patch, using multiscale descriptors and iterative optimization. | Quantifies whether synthesized local neighborhoods resemble geometry already present on the object. | Low coherence error does not prove the missing surface is physically or semantically correct; repeated source patterns can be copied to the wrong place. | [Harary et al. paper](https://www.ee.technion.ac.il/~ayellet/Ps/13-HTG.pdf) |
| **Learned whole-shape completion** | DeepSDF uses Chamfer distance and Earth Mover's distance; IF-Nets uses IoU, Chamfer-L2, and normal consistency for point-cloud/voxel completion. | Measures global agreement to a complete ground-truth shape and, for normals, orientation similarity. | Global averages can hide local source drift, wrong attachment boundaries, small holes, or topology defects; they do not preserve face correspondence. | [DeepSDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/papers/Park_DeepSDF_Learning_Continuous_Signed_Distance_Functions_for_Shape_Representation_CVPR_2019_paper.pdf); [IF-Nets](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/papers/Chibane_Implicit_Functions_in_Feature_Space_for_3D_Shape_Reconstruction_and_CVPR_2020_paper.pdf) |
| **Part-aware inversion fidelity** | SPAGHETTI evaluates inversion with Chamfer distance, Earth Mover's distance, and mesh accuracy before demonstrating edits. | Makes the pre-edit reconstruction error visible instead of attributing all difference to the requested edit. | These aggregate distances still do not guarantee unchanged source faces or exact local boundaries. | [SPAGHETTI paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.13168) |
| **Semantic edit success and visual quality** | Text-guided editors commonly use CLIP text alignment, image metrics, FID, and human preference. Vinedresser3D reports CLIP-T and FID in addition to preservation metrics and a user study. | Measures prompt alignment and perceived quality. | CLIP/FID/human preference do not establish watertightness, manifoldness, feature continuity, or metric source preservation. | [Vinedresser3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.19542); [text-guided 3D editing survey](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10462-024-10937-6) |

The strongest established repair criteria require a watertight, non-self-intersecting output, arbitrary-hole handling, unchanged measured source data, optional user constraints, scalability, and explicit distinction between measured and synthesized regions. [Comparison of hole-filling methods](https://doi.org/10.1515/amcs-2016-0063)

### Inferences

- A defensible evaluation needs separate axes rather than one “mesh quality” score: source-locked unchanged-region displacement/correspondence, boundary-edge and component counts, manifold/self-intersection tests, topology change, seam continuity/curvature, sharp-feature retention, and human or ground-truth judgment of body continuation. The need for separation follows from the incompatible guarantees and metrics used by classical repair, implicit completion, and text-guided editing. [Hole-filling comparison](https://doi.org/10.1515/amcs-2016-0063); [IF-Nets](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/papers/Chibane_Implicit_Functions_in_Feature_Space_for_3D_Shape_Reconstruction_and_CVPR_2020_paper.pdf); [Vinedresser3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.19542)
- Exact-copy checks are stronger than low Chamfer distance for Edit3D's protected source region. Chamfer is set-based and can remain low after remeshing or tangential drift, whereas a retained source-face map can directly detect any changed protected vertex/face. DeepSDF and SPAGHETTI use set distances after reconstruction, while local repair methods can preserve the exterior mesh structurally. [DeepSDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/papers/Park_DeepSDF_Learning_Continuous_Signed_Distance_Functions_for_Shape_Representation_CVPR_2019_paper.pdf); [SPAGHETTI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.13168); [Liepa](https://diglib.eg.org/items/bd5c3658-4de6-4336-a7bd-0baf4f5618ac)
- Natural continuation remains the least standardized criterion. Smoothness, source-patch coherence, ground-truth distance, and human plausibility answer different questions and should not be collapsed into watertightness. [Xu et al.](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~sequin/CS284/PAPERS/Xu_DiscreteSurfaceModPDE.pdf); [Harary et al.](https://www.ee.technion.ac.il/~ayellet/Ps/13-HTG.pdf)

### Gaps

- Most learned completion papers do not report unchanged-region error restricted to observed/source geometry.
- Most text-guided editors do not report open boundary edges, manifoldness, genus, connected components, self-intersections, or sharp-crease continuation on a final mesh.
- Classical hole-filling papers usually lack semantic correctness, multi-instance completeness, and instruction-conditioned body-protection metrics.
- No reviewed metric reliably distinguishes a natural continuation from a smooth but anatomically or mechanically incorrect plug without ground truth or human judgment.

## What risks do diffusion and generative editing methods pose for identity drift and exact source geometry preservation?

### Takeaway

Generative methods trade deterministic source retention for semantic plausibility. Drift can enter before the requested edit through inversion or reconstruction, during editing through stochastic denoising and cross-part attention, and after editing through implicit/voxel/Gaussian-to-mesh decoding. Masks and frozen latent channels reduce spill, but they do not create an exact-copy guarantee for source vertices, topology, UVs, or materials.

### Cited Findings

- **Inversion is already an edit-like reconstruction.** SPAGHETTI must optimize part embeddings for an unseen source and evaluates that inversion with Chamfer distance, EMD, and mesh accuracy; Vinedresser3D inverts the asset into TRELLIS's structured latent before inpainting. Therefore, an output can differ from the source even when the semantic edit is empty. [SPAGHETTI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.13168); [Vinedresser3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.19542)
- **Whole-shape priors can pull out-of-distribution geometry toward a prototype.** IF-Nets reports failure when a shape differs substantially from the training set and notes that a result may resemble a previously seen example. DeepSDF represents a class through a regularized latent code rather than preserving a source mesh instance. [IF-Nets](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/papers/Chibane_Implicit_Functions_in_Feature_Space_for_3D_Shape_Reconstruction_and_CVPR_2020_paper.pdf); [DeepSDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/papers/Park_DeepSDF_Learning_Continuous_Signed_Distance_Functions_for_Shape_Representation_CVPR_2019_paper.pdf)
- **2D-guided editing introduces view inconsistency and occlusion loss.** Vinedresser3D's related-work analysis states that render-edit-reconstruct methods can lose spatial information through occlusion/distortion and suffer multiview inconsistency; its own pipeline still uses a selected edited image as guidance, although final editing occurs in a native 3D latent. [Vinedresser3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.19542)
- **SDS optimization can cause unintended global change.** Vinedresser3D identifies per-scene SDS optimization as expensive and requiring careful tuning to avoid global changes; GS-Edit similarly optimizes Gaussian parameters under diffusion/SDS guidance rather than preserving a source mesh by copy. [Vinedresser3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.19542); [GS-Edit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.05154)
- **Automatic localization materially affects preservation.** Vinedresser3D's human-mask variant improves unedited-region CD from 0.016 to 0.008 and PSNR from 29.45 to 37.69 compared with automatic masks, showing that semantic/localization error propagates into geometry and appearance preservation. [Vinedresser3D evaluation](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.19542)
- **Generation remains the largest observed Vinedresser3D failure source.** In a supplemental decomposition of 31 failed examples, 58.0% were attributed to 3D inversion/generation, versus 22.6% to guidance generation and 19.4% to edit-region detection. This is a failure-case decomposition, not a population failure rate. [CVPR 2026 supplement](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/supplemental/Chi_Vinedresser3D_Towards_Agentic_CVPR_2026_supplemental.pdf)
- **Latent freezing is not geometric identity.** PartRAG and CompoSE preserve or freeze non-target part latents, but the final geometry is decoded from a learned representation; their preservation claim is consequently weaker than retaining the exact source triangles and attributes. [PartRAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17033); [CompoSE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19350)
- **Radiance-field success can be representation-mismatched.** PartNeRF's hard ray-to-part assignment ensures that editing one local NeRF does not change the appearance predicted by other NeRF parts, but its output is a set of radiance fields rather than a watertight editable source mesh. [PartNeRF project](https://ktertikas.github.io/part_nerf)
- **Implicit decoding erases source connectivity.** DeepSDF, IF-Nets, and SPAGHETTI obtain meshes by querying a continuous field and extracting an isosurface, so source face indices and connectivity do not survive the round trip. [DeepSDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/papers/Park_DeepSDF_Learning_Continuous_Signed_Distance_Functions_for_Shape_Representation_CVPR_2019_paper.pdf); [IF-Nets](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/papers/Chibane_Implicit_Functions_in_Feature_Space_for_3D_Shape_Reconstruction_and_CVPR_2020_paper.pdf); [SPAGHETTI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.13168)

### Inferences

- For exact source preservation, the safest architecture is a hard copy of protected source faces plus a separately generated local patch, followed by seam validation. A diffusion mask or frozen latent is useful evidence of locality but is not equivalent to this construction. [Liepa local patching](https://diglib.eg.org/items/bd5c3658-4de6-4336-a7bd-0baf4f5618ac); [Vinedresser3D masked latent editing](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.19542)
- Any generative baseline should be evaluated with an identity/no-op control before judging edit quality; otherwise inversion and decoding drift are confounded with the requested deletion. SPAGHETTI's explicit inversion evaluation and Vinedresser3D's inversion-focused ablations support this requirement. [SPAGHETTI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.13168); [Vinedresser3D supplement](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/supplemental/Chi_Vinedresser3D_Towards_Agentic_CVPR_2026_supplemental.pdf)
- Diffusion is most defensible as a proposal generator for ambiguous local geometry, not as evidence that the proposal is the true exposed body. ShapeFormer explicitly models a distribution of plausible completions, which formalizes ambiguity rather than resolving identity. [ShapeFormer](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Yan_ShapeFormer_Transformer-Based_Shape_Completion_via_Sparse_Representation_CVPR_2022_paper.html)

### Gaps

- Published “preservation” metrics rarely verify exact equality of source vertices, face indices, UV coordinates, normals/tangents, materials, skinning weights, or animation rig bindings.
- Few papers include no-op reconstruction controls, repeated stochastic runs, or worst-case protected-region drift.
- Text-guided benchmarks emphasize semantic alignment and appearance; they do not establish manufacturable topology or downstream editability.
- Latent-space locality has not been shown to imply bounded Euclidean displacement after nonlinear decoding.

## Which methods provide code, checkpoints, and practical licenses?

### Takeaway

Classical implementations offer the clearest deployable licensing: PoissonRecon and a modern Liepa implementation are MIT; MeshFix is GPLv3 or separately licensed for commercial use; Volfill permits free research and commercial use but is old research code. Learned systems often release code and weights, but practical use may still be limited by noncommercial terms, missing licenses, unreleased training components, large model dependencies, or third-party API/model licenses.

### Cited Findings

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PoissonRecon** | Maintained C++ source and executables/build instructions. | Repository license is **MIT**. | Global reconstruction and resampling make it a comparison method, not an exact local source-preserving drop-in. | [Repository](https://github.com/mkazhdan/PoissonRecon); [license](https://github.com/mkazhdan/PoissonRecon/blob/master/LICENSE) |

### Inferences

- The lowest-friction reproducible baselines are local classical methods with permissive code, especially the MIT Liepa implementation and PoissonRecon; however, their scientific roles differ because Liepa is local while PoissonRecon is global. [Liepa implementation](https://github.com/russelmann/hole-filling-liepa); [PoissonRecon](https://github.com/mkazhdan/PoissonRecon)
- MeshFix is technically practical for watertight repair but its GPL/commercial dual licensing is a material product constraint. [MeshFix licensing](https://github.com/MarcoAttene/MeshFix-V2.1)
- A permissive top-level repository license does not automatically clear model weights, training data, APIs, or transitive dependencies. Vinedresser3D is the clearest example because its pipeline orchestrates several separately governed models and services. [Vinedresser3D method](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.19542); [repository](https://github.com/ykchizs/Vinedresser3D)

### Gaps

- Several recent 2025–2026 systems are preprints whose code, checkpoints, exact training data rights, and long-term maintenance could not all be verified.
- Paper/document Creative Commons terms must not be treated as software or model-weight licenses.
- Repositories without an explicit license grant no default permission to copy, modify, or redistribute code; a direct legal audit remains necessary for production use.
- “Code available” does not establish that reported training, proprietary data, hosted models, or API-dependent results are reproducible.

## Where is the genuine research gap relative to Edit3D's decoupled source-face mask plus frozen restoration interface?

### Takeaway

The defensible gap is not “3D part deletion has never been done.” SPAGHETTI, SPLICE, and Vinedresser3D support deletion in learned representations, and classical mesh processing has long filled holes. The narrower gap is a source-preserving, instruction-conditioned pipeline that exports an explicit original-face mask, keeps semantic selection separate from restoration policy, preserves protected source geometry exactly, and evaluates the new local surface for both mesh validity and natural body continuation.

### Cited Findings

- **Classical methods start after localization.** Liepa, PDE fairing, volumetric diffusion, MeshFix, and context-based completion assume a known hole or damaged region; none interprets a natural-language instruction or verifies that all requested attachment instances were selected. [Liepa](https://diglib.eg.org/items/bd5c3658-4de6-4336-a7bd-0baf4f5618ac); [Xu et al.](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~sequin/CS284/PAPERS/Xu_DiscreteSurfaceModPDE.pdf); [Davis et al.](https://graphics.stanford.edu/papers/holefill-3dpvt02/); [MeshFix](https://github.com/MarcoAttene/MeshFix-V2.1); [Harary et al.](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2532548)
- **Learned completion usually discards exact source correspondence.** DeepSDF, IF-Nets, ShapeFormer, and adversarial implicit completion infer complete fields or shapes and then extract a new surface, optimizing global plausibility/accuracy rather than retaining source face IDs. [DeepSDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/papers/Park_DeepSDF_Learning_Continuous_Signed_Distance_Functions_for_Shape_Representation_CVPR_2019_paper.pdf); [IF-Nets](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/papers/Chibane_Implicit_Functions_in_Feature_Space_for_3D_Shape_Reconstruction_and_CVPR_2020_paper.pdf); [ShapeFormer](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Yan_ShapeFormer_Transformer-Based_Shape_Completion_via_Sparse_Representation_CVPR_2022_paper.html); [adversarial implicit completion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.10060)
- **Part-aware generation is editable but representation-changing.** SPAGHETTI, SPLICE, PartRAG, PartGen, and CompoSE expose part structure, but they reconstruct or decode part-aware assets rather than export an immutable source-face deletion mask for a separate restoration consumer. [SPAGHETTI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.13168); [SPLICE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.04514); [PartRAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17033); [PartGen](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/Chen_PartGen_Part-level_3D_Generation_and_Reconstruction_with_Multi-view_Diffusion_Models_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf); [CompoSE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19350)
- **Text-guided editors conflate localization and synthesis more than Edit3D's interface does.** Vinedresser3D identifies edit/preserve regions and then performs TRELLIS latent inpainting in the same generative representation; its automatic localization is evaluated through final preservation metrics rather than an original source-face contract consumed by an independent fixed restoration module. [Vinedresser3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.19542)
- **Radiance-field and Gaussian editors are not sufficient evidence for mesh editing.** Instruct-NeRF2NeRF, Vox-E, GaussianEditor, and GS-Edit optimize view synthesis representations; successful rendered deletion does not establish a watertight, manifold, source-preserving edited mesh. [Instruct-NeRF2NeRF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/html/Haque_Instruct-NeRF2NeRF_Editing_3D_Scenes_with_Instructions_ICCV_2023_paper.html); [Vox-E](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/html/Sella_Vox-E_Text-Guided_Voxel_Editing_of_3D_Objects_ICCV_2023_paper.html); [GaussianEditor](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Chen_GaussianEditor_Swift_and_Controllable_3D_Editing_with_Gaussian_Splatting_CVPR_2024_paper.html); [GS-Edit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.05154)
- **Prior evaluation rarely joins semantic and geometric accountability.** Classical surveys require unchanged measured data and valid topology, while Vinedresser3D reports semantic alignment and unedited-region similarity; neither line evaluates the complete combination of instruction correctness, source-face mask accuracy, exact protected-region identity, watertightness, and natural continuation. [Hole-filling comparison](https://doi.org/10.1515/amcs-2016-0063); [Vinedresser3D evaluation](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.19542)

### Inferences

- Edit3D's potentially novel contribution is the **contract and accountability boundary**: Module 1 is judged on an explicit mask over original source faces, and a fixed Module 2 is judged separately on mask conversion, protected geometry, topology, and continuation. This avoids attributing a generative model's reconstruction drift to masking quality or hiding a bad mask behind a plausible resynthesis. The literature's split between post-hoc classical filling and end-to-end generative editing supports this scoped claim. [Classical survey](https://doi.org/10.1515/amcs-2016-0063); [text-guided editing survey](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10462-024-10937-6)
- The strongest research question is not whether a method can make the removed part disappear in renders, but whether it can identify **all and only** the source faces belonging to the instructed attachment and enable a downstream method to reconstruct a valid, natural body surface without changing protected source geometry. Radiance-field editors and whole-shape generators do not directly answer that question. [Instruct-NeRF2NeRF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/html/Haque_Instruct-NeRF2NeRF_Editing_3D_Scenes_with_Instructions_ICCV_2023_paper.html); [DeepSDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/papers/Park_DeepSDF_Learning_Continuous_Signed_Distance_Functions_for_Shape_Representation_CVPR_2019_paper.pdf)
- Module 2 should remain frozen in the current M1 work. The restoration literature is most useful now for defining deferred comparison classes—local triangulation/fairing, feature-aware/context completion, volumetric/global reconstruction, and generative completion—without changing the active interface or confounding mask experiments. [Liepa](https://diglib.eg.org/items/bd5c3658-4de6-4336-a7bd-0baf4f5618ac); [feature-aware completion](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10851-022-01107-w); [Davis et al.](https://graphics.stanford.edu/papers/holefill-3dpvt02/); [ShapeFormer](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Yan_ShapeFormer_Transformer-Based_Shape_Completion_via_Sparse_Representation_CVPR_2022_paper.html)
- Novelty wording should remain narrow: “decoupled source-face semantic deletion and restoration with explicit preservation accounting” is supportable as a research gap; “first semantic 3D deletion system” is contradicted by SPAGHETTI, SPLICE, and Vinedresser3D. [SPAGHETTI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.13168); [SPLICE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.04514); [Vinedresser3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.19542)

### Gaps

- A literature claim cannot establish that Edit3D's frozen Module 2 produces the correct hidden surface; that requires controlled project evaluation with known or carefully reviewed continuation targets.
- No public benchmark was found that jointly labels instruction semantics, repeated target instances, exact source-face deletion masks, exposed-body ground truth, protected source correspondence, materials, and topology.
- No reviewed method reports a formal guarantee that a learned deletion edit leaves every protected source vertex and attribute bit-identical.
- The natural-continuation target can be genuinely ambiguous when the attachment occludes or replaces body geometry; a benchmark must distinguish uniquely recoverable continuation, policy-defined continuation, and merely plausible completion.
- Recent 2026 preprints should be treated as provisional until publication status, code, checkpoints, licenses, and independent reproduction are established.
