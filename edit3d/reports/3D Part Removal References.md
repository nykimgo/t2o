# Preserve Candidates, Diagnose Failures, Refine Boundaries

## Executive decision summary

**The most useful references do not support replacing Edit3D with one end-to-end model; they support a staged, source-face-accountable pipeline.** For current Step 6 M1 Phase 6.4, the highest-value combination is: preserve native 3D alternatives from P3-SAM and PartField for **G1**; use Find3D-style text relevance, Search3D-style parent/part context, and PartSLIP++-style cross-view consistency for **G2**; test face-graph optimization, hierarchy alternatives, Point-SAM, and cropped SAM 2 as bounded **G3** corrections; and use explicit visibility/disagreement evidence, not generic confidence, to choose `RESELECT`, `OBSERVE`, `REFINE`, or `UNRESOLVED` in **E4**. This direction agrees with the ten-case project evidence: candidate capacity and automatic selection remain far apart, richer candidates can recover some cases, and one local refiner cannot recover an omitted distant instance. Published benchmark scores are not directly comparable because they span class-agnostic decomposition, semantic part IoU, scene-instance AP, video J&F, and rendered-edit quality on different datasets. The report therefore ranks references by architectural relevance to Edit3D, not by their reported headline metric. **Candidate expansion is not an additional camera observation; semantic selection is not geometric segmentation; candidate capacity is not automatic success; a manually localized production repair is not zero-box automation; and a successful source-face mask is not a successful restored mesh.** The frozen baselines, Module 2, and `MaskInterfaceContract` should remain unchanged.

## Evidence boundaries expose the highest-risk claims

This synthesis uses four evidence labels. **Peer-reviewed/published evidence** includes venue papers such as PartField, Find3D, Open3DIS, Search3D, PS3, GeoSAM2, SATR, and Vinedresser3D. **Preprint evidence** includes P3-SAM, PartSLIP++, SAMesh, Point-SAM, OpenPart3D, the Bayesian next-best-view work, and recent generative systems unless a primary venue record was verified. **Official software documentation** establishes an exposed operator, API, repository, or stated license, but not scientific quality or reproducibility on Edit3D. **Project-specific hypotheses** are recommendations inferred from those sources and the ten confirmed-patch diagnostics; they are not published findings or authorized implementation decisions.

The notes are internally consistent on the central technical distinctions. P3-SAM supplies alternative geometric masks but no text grounding; PartField supplies hierarchy but no text head; Find3D supplies text relevance but no instance hierarchy; and multiview systems supply observation and association evidence but not source-face removal guarantees. They also consistently report that P3-SAM's three outputs are **alternative scale-diverse predictions, not a fixed semantic fine/medium/coarse ordering**, and that automatic predicted-IoU ranking is a mask-quality estimate rather than instruction correctness. The ten-case project synthesis is equally narrow: six cases have a witness in either final partition, seven when cached intermediate proposals are included, while cached selectors reach at most two of ten at the scoped gate. These are evaluator-assisted development-patch diagnostics with unknown faces, not seven full-object successes, not an unseen benchmark, and not an extrapolated 50-case rate.

Several claims carry elevated legal or scientific risk:

1. **P3-SAM deployment is presently blocked by its published territory clause in the South Korean project context unless separate authorization is obtained.** The [Tencent license](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-Part/main/LICENSE) also restricts some output uses and adds scale-related terms. This is a technical inventory, not legal advice, but the restriction must not be hidden behind code availability.
2. **PartField is a research comparator, not an unrestricted production dependency.** Its [official license](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nv-tlabs/PartField/main/LICENSE) limits use and derivatives to non-commercial research and education, and the stronger PartNet-trained checkpoint is withheld.
3. **Repository visibility is not deployment permission.** Search3D and SAMesh had no verified root software license in the reviewed primary material; OpenPart3D and PS3 had no verified official runnable release at the cutoff. Their concepts remain useful, but availability must be labeled `unverified` rather than guessed.
4. **Published scores are protocol-dependent.** GeoSAM2 retains the best result from four prompt views and automatically segments an opposite unlabeled region; PartField's connectivity-assisted result can use connected components and best hierarchy-level selection; OpenMask3D/Open3DIS report proposal confidence fixed at 1.0. None is a deployable confidence estimate for Edit3D.
5. **“Open vocabulary” does not mean arbitrary instruction logic.** ConceptFusion explicitly reports weak compositionality and no negation understanding, and OpenPart3D reports materially poorer implicit-query than direct-query results ([ConceptFusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.07241); [OpenPart3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.19331)).
6. **“First 3D deletion” would be false.** SPAGHETTI, SPLICE, and Vinedresser3D already support deletion in learned representations. The defensible gap is narrower: instruction-conditioned original-face masking, exact protected-source accounting, and separately evaluated local restoration.

## G1 should preserve geometric alternatives before semantic pruning

G1's purpose is candidate capacity, not semantic correctness. **P3-SAM is the most direct reference because one positive 3D point yields three alternatives, automatic mode oversamples prompts, and the official pipeline ranks and suppresses masks before returning a partition.** Edit3D should treat pre-ranking/pre-NMS masks as a versioned candidate bank and preserve the exact sampled-point-to-source-face provenance. Its documented face-voting and flood-fill path is useful, but flood filling means source-face mapping is not automatically lossless ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.06784); [official auto-mask code](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-Part/blob/main/P3-SAM/demo/auto_mask.py)). **PartField complements this with registered hierarchy cuts**: several granularity levels can expose a seam hidden by one final clustering, but best-level oracle selection is capacity evidence, not automatic performance ([paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Liu_PartField_Learning_3D_Feature_Fields_for_Part_Segmentation_and_Beyond_ICCV_2025_paper.html); [official code](https://github.com/nv-tlabs/PartField)).

Open3DIS supplies the strongest general design precedent for unioning heterogeneous proposal sources before deduplication: on its ScanNet200 scene task, adding 2D-guided proposals to 3D proposals increased proposal recall from 61.63% to 73.29% ([paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/papers/Nguyen_Open3DIS_Open-Vocabulary_3D_Instance_Segmentation_with_2D_Mask_Guidance_CVPR_2024_paper.pdf)). That gain is not transferable as an Edit3D number, but it supports preserving complementary native-3D and lifted-2D hypotheses. PS3 similarly supports texture-aware masklets for geometrically similar repeated parts, yet its mixed strict-AP versus tolerant-AP results show that better coarse proposal recall can coexist with poorer exact boundaries ([paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026/papers/Yen_PS3_Part_Level_Instance_Segmentation_in_3D_WACV_2026_paper.pdf)). G1 should therefore optimize and report **oracle recall/capacity at a fixed pool version**, source-face mapping loss, hierarchy/head provenance, overlap, and filtering loss—not claim that more candidates improve the final mask.

Candidate expansion and observation must remain explicit separate actions. Exposing another P3-SAM head, PartField hierarchy level, prompt threshold, or 2D mask on the same pixels adds a **hypothesis**. Rendering a calibrated new camera adds **evidence** and can also incidentally reveal a new 2D hypothesis. Neither operation guarantees success: a retained mask can be wrong, and a new view can provide redundant evidence. This distinction is foundational for E4 because a confidently wrong semantic choice needs `RESELECT`, while a target that is genuinely under-observed may benefit from `OBSERVE`.

## G2 must select a complete, safe set rather than a plausible single mask

G2 is a constrained semantic set problem over G1 candidates. Find3D provides a useful permissive text-relevance channel because it maps points and free-form part text into a shared SigLIP space, but its scores are similarities, not calibrated probabilities, and repeated-part activation does not produce stable instance identities ([paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Ma_Find_Any_Part_in_3D_ICCV_2025_paper.pdf)). Search3D shows why part-only similarity is incomplete: combining parent-object and child-part context improves its own hierarchy search, while PS3 shows that texture-aware instance proposals can help when geometry alone merges repeated coplanar parts. PartSLIP++ contributes a distinct signal—multi-view identity consistency via EM and Hungarian matching. These ingredients support separate channels for entity relevance, requested-instance/count coverage, parent compatibility, view agreement, and body risk. **They do not establish one published Edit3D objective or weighting.**

The selector should interpret the full instruction as set constraints: `one`, `both`, `all`, spatial qualifiers, conjunctions, and exclusions. A high-quality part partition can still select the wrong entity; a correct single candidate can still omit a bilateral counterpart; and a large feasible union can still damage protected body. G2 should therefore report source-area target recall `R`, selected-area contamination `C`, protected-area damage `D`, entirely missed instances, unknown-area selection, and acceptance coverage separately. The project example in which low contamination coexists with high protected-area damage demonstrates why `C` cannot replace `D`. No reviewed public benchmark evaluates this complete instruction-conditioned safety tuple.

The strongest project hypothesis is a candidate graph rather than a flat mask list. Nodes retain source faces, generator, hierarchy/head, prompt/view provenance, semantic scores, quality scores, and overlaps; edges retain parent-child, split/merge, cross-view correspondence, conflict, and possible symmetry. A G2 set objective can then reward instruction relevance, instance completeness, parent compatibility, and multiview support while penalizing body risk, redundant overlap, unsupported islands, and boundary instability. This is an auditable hypothesis for E2, not a measured solution, and the runner-up set should remain available when the margin is small.

## G3 should correct only localized, evidenced boundary failures

The most defensible G3 baseline is a **local source-face graph** around the current boundary. Binary graph cut gives the exact optimum of a registered submodular region-plus-boundary energy; random walker exposes soft seed-conditioned probabilities; CRF/Potts terms can add appearance or feature coupling; SDF, concavity, dihedral angle, and neck loops can propose likely seams ([graph cut](https://www.csd.uwo.ca/~yboykov/Papers/iccv01.pdf); [random walker](https://doi.org/10.1109/TPAMI.2006.233); [CGAL SDF](https://doc.cgal.org/latest/Surface_mesh_segmentation/index.html)). These are valuable because they act directly on source faces and can preserve the downstream contract. Their energies describe the registered features and seeds, not semantic truth. A graph optimum can confidently sharpen the wrong entity, erase a thin target, or choose a short cut through the body.

Frozen promptable systems add complementary capacity. Point-SAM offers permissive native-3D point prompting; P3-SAM all-head retention tests hidden alternatives; PartField local hierarchy cuts test existing granularity; cropped SAM 2 supports positive and negative 2D points at higher visible resolution. For cropped 2D refinement, only depth-consistent visible pixels should vote to original faces, and unseen faces must remain unknown until another registered observation supports them. A crop around the wrong selected component cannot find an omitted distant target. This is why E3 must report three modes separately: evaluator-supplied `diagnostic_location`, evaluator best-choice `diagnostic_mask_choice`, and deployable `automatic_location_and_choice`.

G3 eligibility should depend on paired evidence, not mean IoU alone. Every correction family needs already-correct controls, wrong-localization controls, fixed local domains, fixed prompt budgets, and per-case deltas in `R/C/D`, instance completeness, boundary quality, harmful-action rate, runtime, and memory. A finite failed menu establishes only that the registered methods failed; it does not prove that no boundary method could succeed. Conversely, a diagnostic success with automatic-location failure points back to G2/observation rather than proving a deployable refiner.

## E4 should route by failure evidence, not one confidence score

No reviewed scalar distinguishes semantic selection error, observation insufficiency, and candidate-boundary insufficiency. Predicted IoU estimates mask overlap quality; graph energy estimates fit to an engineered objective; random-walker/CRF entropy depends on seeds and features; VLM agreement can reflect shared error; and low semantic entropy can be confidently wrong. The Bayesian next-best-view preprint directly illustrates the last failure: entropy-only acquisition can miss a view that changes an already confident wrong prediction, while task-specific change-sensitive acquisition is more robust ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05095)). The E4 sidecar should therefore keep at least three confidence layers separate: semantic/entity/count support, observation sufficiency, and boundary/candidate adequacy.

The bounded action logic should follow the evidence:

| Action | Evidence that supports testing it | Evidence against it | Required interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ACCEPT` | Entity/count checks pass; visibility is adequate; no supported spill or better registered alternative; frozen acceptance operating point passes | Conflicting modalities, low coverage, unstable boundary, or unsupported calibration | A fallible accepted output, never a proof of correctness |
| `RESELECT` | A registered alternative improves entity/count, multiview support, and body-risk evidence by a frozen margin | No credible alternative, or disputed area is a localized seam shared by all candidates | Reconsider the full bank; do not infer pool sufficiency from one bad selection |
| `OBSERVE` | Low visible-view count/projected area, depth occlusion, or view-subset disagreement; a candidate camera is predicted to expose disputed faces | Several clear views agree on the wrong entity or every candidate straddles the same boundary | Add camera evidence, not merely another mask on the same pixels |
| `REFINE` | Identity/location is supported and visibility is adequate, but checked candidates consistently spill or omit at a localized boundary | Missing distant instance, weak localization, or purely semantic disagreement | Invoke only an E3 action with measured recovery, regression, and cost |
| `UNRESOLVED` | Conflicting evidence, unsupported calibration, failed/unsafe actions, or exhausted budget | None; abstention is a valid safety outcome | Count as nonsuccess; withhold from automatic Module 2 execution |

Visibility-aware lifting from SATR and GeoSAM2 supports logging visible projected area, depth consistency, and view count per face. SAMesh shows that independent per-view proposals can be safer than assuming video-like camera continuity, while GeoSAM2's geometry adaptation and full-view memory show how large view jumps violate vanilla SAM 2 assumptions. A practical E4 camera policy should initially rank a finite registered view pool by expected newly visible disputed area, reduction in cross-view disagreement, and semantic utility, minus redundancy. This is a project hypothesis; no cited work validates it for instruction-conditioned source-face removal.

Selective reporting is essential. Accuracy among accepted masks must be paired with acceptance coverage, unresolved rate, false acceptance, harmful-action rate, risk-coverage curves, and action cost. A controller that abstains on nearly every case is not successful, and a second call to the same VLM is not independent verification. Evaluator-only E1 capacity labels and best-action outcomes remain outside runtime inference.

## Deferred Module 2 references define comparisons, not current changes

The removal/restoration literature supports Edit3D's decoupled architecture. Classical methods such as Liepa hole filling, PDE fairing, volumetric diffusion, MeshFix, and context-based completion begin **after** a boundary or hole is supplied; they do not identify the instructed attachment ([Liepa](https://diglib.eg.org/items/bd5c3658-4de6-4336-a7bd-0baf4f5618ac); [PDE fairing](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~sequin/CS284/PAPERS/Xu_DiscreteSurfaceModPDE.pdf); [context completion](https://www.ee.technion.ac.il/~ayellet/Ps/13-HTG.pdf)). Learned systems such as DeepSDF, IF-Nets, SPAGHETTI, and Vinedresser3D can complete or delete geometry, but they invert, regenerate, or remesh a learned representation rather than retain original face identity ([DeepSDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/papers/Park_DeepSDF_Learning_Continuous_Signed_Distance_Functions_for_Shape_Representation_CVPR_2019_paper.pdf); [SPAGHETTI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.13168); [Vinedresser3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.19542)).

This evidence reinforces three separate claims. **A watertight cap is not necessarily a natural continuation. A correct mask is not proof that Module 2 reconstructs the right hidden body. Low Chamfer or high render similarity is not exact protected-face preservation.** Vinedresser3D is especially informative because its human-mask condition improves unedited-region metrics relative to its automatic mask, showing that localization errors propagate downstream; however, its latent inpainting and decoded output are not a source-face-preserving restoration baseline. Module 2 should therefore remain frozen during M1 experiments. Later compatibility work should separately report source-face mask quality, source-face-to-contract conversion loss, protected geometry identity, topology/watertightness, and natural continuation.

Production comparisons need automation labels: **A0** receives only source plus instruction; **A1** receives user/evaluator localization and automatic repair; **A2** permits expert manual editing. Blender/Houdini/PyMeshLab with a supplied face mask, Rodin with a user region, and ZBrush sculpting must not be presented as equivalent competitors to Edit3D A0. The fairest production panel uses the same supplied oracle source-face mask for A1/A2 tools and reports operator time, retries, watertightness, continuity, protected-region drift, topology/UV/material preservation, and automation level.

## What to test next, mapped to E1–E4

The following is a decision-ready test plan, **not authorization to change code, launch GPU inference, train models, promote a baseline, modify Module 2, add case-specific rules, or alter the mask interface**.

| Stage | Next registered question | Evidence-preserving test | Decision criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **E1 — candidate capacity / G1** | Do existing final and cached intermediate pools express the confirmed target patches? | Inventory immutable P3-SAM heads/pre-NMS proposals and PartField hierarchy cuts with exact source-face provenance; report final versus expanded pools separately; preserve timeout and unknown labels | A feasible witness proves finite-pool capacity; only completed exact infeasibility proves finite-pool insufficiency; automatic success remains unclaimed |
| **E2 — set selection / G2** | Can source-only semantics and multiview evidence close the measured selector-capacity gap on a fixed pool? | Replay Find3D and legacy MV controls; compare one preregistered set selector using full instruction, count/instance constraints, parent-part relevance, visibility, overlap, and body-risk channels; keep candidate bank and views fixed | Paired improvement in strict mask success, instance completeness, `R/C/D`, and risk/coverage without hidden filtering loss; otherwise retain the simpler control |
| **E3 — boundary feasibility / G3** | When location is correct, can a bounded action repair an unsupported seam without damaging good masks? | Under identical diagnostic points/regions and budgets, compare no correction, face graph cut, random walker, hierarchy alternative, Point-SAM/P3-SAM alternatives where legally available, and cropped SAM 2 lifting; include good-mask and wrong-location controls | An action becomes E4-eligible only after measured recoveries, good-mask regressions, automatic-location gap, and fixed cost/parameter bounds |
| **E4 — diagnosis and action control** | Can source-only evidence choose `ACCEPT/RESELECT/OBSERVE/REFINE/UNRESOLVED` better than simpler same-budget policies? | Compare no correction, always-apply actions, a fixed retry policy, diagnosis-driven routing, and evaluator-only finite best-action oracle; use a finite additional-view pool and retain separate semantic/visibility/boundary diagnostics | Higher strict success at transparent coverage/cost, no observed severe protected-body regression in the registered confirmation scope, and explicit unresolved counts; no claim of guaranteed safety |

If separately authorized, E1 should use the existing confirmed-patch evidence without asking the user to repeat blanket annotation. E2 must not change candidate capacity while claiming a selector-only gain. E3 must not use evaluator boundaries as inference input or infer an omitted distant instance from a local crop. E4 must not convert `UNRESOLVED` or an empty mask into successful removal. Any later fixed-Module-2 compatibility check remains a separate integration evaluation after M1 evidence is stable.

## Conclusion

The remaining research gap is precise and consequential: **no verified system jointly converts a free-form removal instruction into a complete, safe set of original source faces; distinguishes semantic, observation, and boundary failures; applies a bounded correction; and then enables separately accountable natural surface restoration while preserving protected source geometry.** Existing work supplies strong components, not the complete contract.

For Phase 6.4, the defensible path is to measure the gaps between retained candidate capacity, automatic set selection, conditional boundary correction, and bounded action routing. That framing preserves scientific attribution and the frozen architecture: G1/G2/G3 remain upstream of the unchanged mask interface, and Module 2 remains a separate deferred question rather than a hidden explanation for masking success or failure.
